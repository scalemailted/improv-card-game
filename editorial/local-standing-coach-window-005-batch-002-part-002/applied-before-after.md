## D02-single-b — revised

Create reasons another player must stay with you. Never directly ask them to stay.

Mechanism: A makes each attempted departure solution create another small obligation requiring B's presence.

Confusable concept: Protective fussing could resemble simply correcting someone's packing.

### before

**A:** You've packed your suitcase upside down. Let me show you.

**B:** A suitcase doesn't have an upside down.

**A:** Then explain these shoes facing the handle.

**B:** I need to leave.

**A:** Of course. After we settle the shoes. It's a long journey.

### after

**A:** Before you zip your suitcase, let me wrap that jar of jam.

**B:** It's already wrapped in my jumper.

**A:** That's your train jumper. We'll need to unpack it and find something else.

**B:** I'll leave the jam here.

**A:** Then you haven't tasted it. I'll get a spoon before you decide.
