## S142-single-a — revised

Accept that a secret plan exists, then focus on schedules, witnesses, explanations, cleanup, and what everyone must remember.

Mechanism: A accepts secrecy and continually translates the plan into practical requirements for maintaining its cover.

Confusable concept: Ordinary logistical fussing concerns transport efficiency; this card concerns what observers will notice and believe.

### before

**A:** Fine, we're planning a secret picnic. Who explains the rented bus?

**B:** We'll say it's a school trip.

**A:** On Sunday, with forty adults and a cake?

**B:** We'll use cars instead.

**A:** Good. Now whose drive hides forty adults' cars?

### after

**A:** For Dad's surprise picnic, where can forty guests board the bus unseen?

**B:** Outside my shop. Dad never goes there.

**A:** Good. Tell everyone to arrive separately so the neighbours don't mention a crowd.

**B:** Dad's offered to open the shop for me while I organise it.

**A:** Then board at the library. Ring all forty guests; no group message Dad might see. Who's collecting him after we leave?

First / second pass: 92 / 86

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 accepts the secret picnic; A3 tests its explanation and A5 addresses concealing cars. Result 4 — A1 accepts the surprise picnic; A3 manages witnesses, and A5 coordinates relocation, discreet notification and Dad's collection.
- responseLogic: original 5 — B2's school trip causes A3's Sunday objection; B4's cars cause A5's parking question. Result 5 — B2 supplies the shop as a concealed boarding point, prompting A3's staggered arrivals; B4 puts Dad there, directly requiring A5's relocation.
- relationship: original 4 — B4 changes the transport plan constructively, letting A5 tackle its new concealment problem. Result 4 — B2 helps organise the surprise, while B4 reveals that Dad's helpful offer compromises their arrangement and changes A's options.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 turns B4's solution into the larger problem of hiding forty adults' cars. Result 4 — B4 turns their safe location into an exposure risk; A5 expands the response to forty individual calls and a separate collection arrangement.
- specificity: original 5 — A1's secret picnic and rented bus become Sunday's forty adults and cake in A3. Result 5 — A1 immediately establishes forty guests, a bus and Dad's surprise picnic; B4 gives the shop opening a concrete family explanation.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's compact list punctures the school-trip cover; A5 earns its logistical complication. Result 4 — A3's concern about neighbours develops into A5's forty telephone calls: an earned logistical burden, although the last turn packs in several tasks.

B4 causes A5: B4 places Dad at the boarding location; A5 changes the location and specifies how to notify guests without exposing the surprise.

Alternatives: The preserved alternative audits a retrospective cake-delivery alibi against transport and witness evidence. This candidate manages a large group's prospective movements and adapts to Dad's helpful intervention.

Reservation: The behaviour is close to competent surprise-party planning, so the comic excess is modest. No rewrite needed for an internal pass.

## S143-single-a — revised

Choose one ordinary detail and treat it as the clue that will expose the entire hidden story.

Mechanism: A makes one ordinary trace the privileged clue through which the whole hidden event must be explained.

Confusable concept: General investigation follows multiple relevant clues; this stance gives one selected detail disproportionate explanatory power.

### before

**A:** Ignore the missing painting for a moment. Why is that thread red?

**B:** It's from my scarf.

**A:** Then why is it on the empty frame?

**B:** I brushed against it.

**A:** Good. The thread has finally acquired a witness.

### after

**A:** The painting's gone. That red thread on its frame will explain everything.

**B:** My scarf caught there last night. Can we open the gallery?

**A:** This thread will date the theft. Was the painting there then?

**B:** Yes. But I picked that thread off before leaving.

**A:** Then this one means someone came back wearing your scarf. Keep it on; we'll reconstruct the whole night from this snag.

First / second pass: 78 / 86

- cardFidelity: original 4 — A1 isolates red thread and A3 pursues its location, but A5 does not connect it to the entire theft. Result 5 — A1 declares that one red thread will explain everything; A3 and A5 keep treating that ordinary detail as the route to the entire theft.
- responseLogic: original 5 — B2's scarf leads to A3's location question; B4's contact makes B the witness in A5. Result 4 — B2 identifies a scarf snag, which A3 uses to date the disappearance; B4 says that thread was removed, prompting A5's return-visit theory.
- relationship: original 3 — B2 and B4 answer credibly but reveal no competing need beyond explaining the thread. Result 4 — B2 wants to open the gallery and supplies an innocent explanation; B4 complicates the evidence, and A5 recruits B into a reconstruction.
- heightening: original 3 — A5 renames B a witness without increasing the thread's explanatory reach. Result 4 — After B4 removes the original thread from the timeline, A5 escalates from dating the theft to reconstructing the whole night through the scarf.
- specificity: original 4 — A1's missing painting and A3's empty frame provide a clear investigative setting. Result 4 — A1 establishes a missing painting and a thread on its frame; B2 adds the gallery-opening pressure and the scarf's contact with the frame.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A5's thread acquiring a witness is compact and tied to B4's admission. Result 4 — A3's practical timing question becomes A5's excessive commitment to reconstructing everything from a snag, without introducing a separate joke.

B4 causes A5: B4 establishes removal of the earlier thread; A5 interprets the present thread as evidence of a second scarf visit.

Alternatives: This alternative pressures a gallery colleague through a theft reconstruction. The coffee-ring alternative involves domestic departure pressure and changes the mystery after the keys are found.

Reservation: A5 assumes the same scarf produced the replacement thread and that B currently wears it. The first leap is card-driven; the second is a small staging convenience. No rewrite needed for an internal pass.

## S145-single-a — revised

Confusable concept: Impossible Specialist specifies technical handling; this card chiefly protects an authoritative account of what the event means.

**A:** An advanced demonstration.

**B:** Should we chase it?

**A:** Escort it. Vocabulary matters when dealing with liberated furniture.

### after

**A:** We'll escort it. Ask politely for your uniform; this is a free wardrobe.

**A:** That's a proposal, not a demand. Bring our tenancy agreement; a free wardrobe can negotiate its share of the bedroom.

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 rejects escape as an interpretation; A5 corrects B4's chasing into escorting. Result 5 — A1 declares movement a freedom; A3 corrects stopping into escorting; A5 corrects rent extraction into a negotiable proposal.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 absorbs B2's distance as an advanced demonstration; A5 specifically corrects B4's proposed chase. Result 5 — B2's departing uniform prompts A3's polite retrieval; B4's rent request prompts A5's tenancy negotiation within the same freedom doctrine.
- relationship: original 4 — B4 seeks an actionable response, and A5 supplies one while retaining interpretive authority. Result 4 — B2 urgently needs work clothes and help stopping the wardrobe; A3 offers a constrained solution, while B4 introduces its financial demand.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 extends the preferred explanation into how both characters should approach the wardrobe. Result 4 — A5 turns B4's rent demand into a claim on the bedroom agreement, extending the wardrobe's autonomy into household obligations.
- specificity: original 4 — B2 locates the escaping wardrobe halfway down the road, making the impossible problem concrete. Result 5 — A1's escaping wardrobe and B2's work clothes establish impossible action and ordinary stakes; A5 supplies a concrete tenancy document.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A5's chase/escort correction follows A1's freedom language, though its last sentence explains the joke. Result 4 — A3's 'this is a free wardrobe' sustains A1's interpretation; A5's proposal correction earns its joke through the same legalistic sincerity.

B4 causes A5: B4 reports rent for shirt storage; A5 insists it is a proposal and introduces the household tenancy agreement.

Alternatives: The preserved doorway alternative defends household ownership against a past self. This candidate grants autonomy to a possession and converts retrieval into bargaining.

Reservation: No rewrite needed in this review. B4 reports the wardrobe's demand more than advancing B's own tactic, and the final tenancy phrasing is slightly elaborate.

## S146-single-b — revised

Project absolute certainty about what happens next while quietly revising the prophecy whenever reality disagrees.

Mechanism: A repeatedly adjusts a prophecy's meaning while continuing to announce its imminent fulfilment with certainty.

Confusable concept: A retrospective excuse only explains failure; this stance also sustains certainty about what will happen next.

### before

**A:** A great visitor will arrive wearing gold today.

**B:** Only the plumber came.

**A:** Brass fittings. A regional form of gold.

**B:** He left already.

**A:** As foretold by the phrase great efficiency.

### after

**A:** Set our best plates out. A visitor wearing gold will bring us a fortune tonight.

**B:** It's the plumber with brass fittings. I'll get his payment.

**A:** Working gold. Leave the money; he'll be bringing ours.

**B:** His bill's enormous. We owe him.

**A:** A fortune changing hands. Fetch the chequebook; once he leaves, we'll never have to pay a plumber again.

First / second pass: 70 / 90

- cardFidelity: original 4 — A1 predicts a golden visitor and A3 redefines gold, but A5 becomes retrospective without a next prediction. Result 5 — A1 confidently predicts gold and a fortune; A3 recasts brass as 'Working gold', and A5 silently changes receiving money into money changing hands.
- responseLogic: original 3 — A3 uses B2's plumber; A5 invents 'great efficiency,' a phrase absent from A1's prophecy. Result 5 — B2's plumber and brass fittings directly supply A3's reinterpretation; B4's enormous bill causes A5 to reverse the payment direction and fetch the chequebook.
- relationship: original 3 — B2 and B4 correct the record without expressing a stake in the visitor's arrival. Result 4 — B2 intends to pay the plumber and B4 insists they owe him; A3 briefly obstructs payment before A5 incorporates B's practical solution into the prophecy.
- heightening: original 3 — A5 protects the prediction from B4's departure but does not increase its future commitment. Result 4 — B4 forces A5 to accept a costly contradiction, which A covers with an even broader certainty that they will never pay a plumber again.
- specificity: original 4 — A3's brass fittings tie the celestial claim to B2's ordinary plumber. Result 4 — A1's best plates establish expectant hospitality, while B2's brass fittings and B4's bill turn the visitor into a concrete household expense.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's 'regional form of gold' is a concise, character-driven reinterpretation. Result 4 — A3's 'Working gold' and A5's 'A fortune changing hands' compactly expose the revisions; the last prediction continues the same self-protective logic.

B4 causes A5: B4 establishes the large debt; A5 broadens the prophecy to cover outgoing money and authorises the payment B sought.

Alternatives: The preserved telescope alternative postpones fulfilment through increasingly strained time-zone explanations. This candidate revises material and financial meanings while a household partner successfully gets a bill paid.

Reservation: The final lifelong prediction is broader and less quietly evasive than the earlier revisions, but it remains grounded in the plumber's bill. No rewrite needed for an internal pass.

## S148-single-b — revised

Act as though effortless acceptance of the bizarre premise proves you belong, and treat visible confusion as a personal risk.

Mechanism: A protects insider status by presenting a mistaken response to the bizarre as established familiarity and personal recognition.

Confusable concept: Generic face-saving could concern any mistake; this card makes acceptance of an impossible custom the test of belonging.

### before

**A:** Of course I know the cloud salute. This hand means welcome.

**B:** It means release the rain.

**A:** The previous clouds used it differently.

**B:** We're getting soaked.

**A:** An enthusiastic response. Clearly they recognise me.

### after

**A:** You can introduce me to your cloud family. I know the salute: this hand means welcome.

**B:** That's rain. Shall I tell them you're new?

**A:** No need. The previous clouds used it differently. Your family will appreciate the traditional greeting.

**B:** Mother's raining directly on you. Come under my umbrella.

**A:** And refuse her welcome? Hold my phone; I'll greet your father with both hands.

First / second pass: 90 / 93

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 asserts prior knowledge; A3 invokes previous clouds and A5 treats the mistake as personal recognition. Result 5 — A1 claims knowledge of the cloud salute; A3 rejects being identified as new; A5 accepts getting soaked to preserve belonging.
- responseLogic: original 5 — B2's corrected salute causes A3's historical excuse; B4's soaking becomes A5's enthusiastic response. Result 5 — B2's rain correction becomes A3's traditional-greeting defence; B4's umbrella offer becomes A5's refusal to reject Mother's welcome.
- relationship: original 4 — B4 shares the wet consequence and challenges A's attempt to preserve insider status. Result 4 — B2 offers an introduction that could excuse ignorance; B4 offers shelter. A's status anxiety transforms both helpful offers into risks.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 treats worsening physical consequences as stronger evidence that A belongs. Result 5 — After B4 reports direct rain, A5 refuses shelter and doubles the greeting with both hands, increasing bodily commitment to claimed fluency.
- specificity: original 4 — A1's hand salute causes the rain identified in B2 and experienced in B4. Result 4 — A1 establishes meeting B's cloud family; B4's umbrella and A5's phone give actors clear practical choices despite loosely defined salute mechanics.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A5's 'Clearly they recognise me' completes the social defence without explanatory padding. Result 4 — A5's 'And refuse her welcome?' directly reuses A3's greeting defence; handing over the phone makes the escalation concrete and economical.

B4 causes A5: B4 offers shelter from Mother's rain; A5 treats shelter as rejecting the welcome and escalates the greeting to Father.

Alternatives: The preserved invisible-guest alternative protects membership through ceremonial reinterpretation. This candidate seeks acceptance by B's family and refuses practical protection to maintain that claim.

Reservation: No rewrite needed in this review. The precise relationship between the hand signal and rainfall remains loose, but the status choice is clear.

## S150-single-a — revised

**B:** We're starving, and the soup's floating out of reach. Let me use it.
