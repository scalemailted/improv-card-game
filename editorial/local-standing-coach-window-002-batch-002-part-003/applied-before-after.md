## S96-single-b — revised

Treat every coincidence, interruption, and repeated encounter as proof that this connection is meant to continue.

Mechanism: A reads an accidental coat mix-up and its practical resolution as successive arrangements keeping an old connection alive.

Confusable concept: Generic flirting would use the coat as an excuse without treating each new circumstance as external proof.

### before

**A:** We both reached for this coat. The universe wants us talking.

**B:** It's my coat.

**A:** And somehow my hand found your conversation starter.

**B:** Could I have it back?

**A:** Certainly. Let's see where the sleeves lead us.

### after

**A:** We both reached for this coat. First reunion in ten years, and the universe won't let us leave.

**B:** It's mine. Yours is still on the rack.

**A:** Then it borrowed your coat to stop me missing you.

**B:** Here's yours. Come on, we can share the taxi queue.

**A:** Coats, now taxis. It's arranging this in stages. Where are you headed?
