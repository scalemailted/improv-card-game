## S157-single-b — revised

Be sincerely impressed by how naturally someone inhabits the strange reality, then compete to appear even less surprised.

Mechanism: A admires B's social ease with an invisible guest and then competes by offering even more unhesitating familiarity.

Confusable concept: Bluffing prior acquaintance can resemble the scene, but the card specifically starts from sincere admiration and escalates apparent lack of surprise.

### before

**A:** You've greeted the invisible guest so casually. I do that constantly.

**B:** You greeted the umbrella stand.

**A:** They're often close friends.

**B:** The guest is behind you.

**A:** I knew. I was including their furniture.

### after

**A:** You greet invisible guests so easily. I wish I had your touch. Actually, I usually hug them.

**B:** That's the umbrella stand. Martin's behind you.

**A:** Martin! No formalities between us. Come for supper.

**B:** I usually ask whether he can eat visible food first.

**A:** Oh, we can skip all that. Martin, help yourself from the fridge whenever. I'll get you a key.

- relationship: original 3 — B2 and B4 help locate the guest, though B's role stays limited to correcting A's mistakes. Result 4 — B2 corrects A's mistaken greeting and B4 offers considerate guest knowledge, exposing the gap A tries to surpass.
- heightening: original 3 — A5 broadens the excuse to furniture but repeats A3's face-saving tactic without a new social commitment. Result 5 — A5 expands A3's supper invitation into anytime food access and a house key to seem more naturally familiar than B.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 establishes an invisible guest and B2 supplies a clear mistaken greeting target. Result 4 — B2's umbrella stand and named invisible guest make the correction playable; A5 supplies concrete household stakes.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's close friendship with furniture is concise and A5 recalls it, although that callback dominates the card. Result 4 — A1's 'Actually' reveals the competitive pivot; A5's key offer earns its excess from avoiding B4's careful questions.
