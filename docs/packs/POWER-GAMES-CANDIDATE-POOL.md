## Selection principles

The selected set favors holder-specific behavior, visible power tactics, repeatable escalation, broad setting portability, and clear separation from Core Foundations and Everyday Friction. A Power Games card should answer **who gets to decide, what makes that authority real, and how the holder behaves when power is uncertain**.

Held candidates remain available for revision or reassignment. Rejected candidates should not advance in their current form because they are redundant, overly literal, overly coercive, setting-bound, or weaker than the selected card occupying the same subtheme.

This is an editorial drafting record. Independent blind reads and repeated live troupe performance remain required before the pack can move from `playtest` to `published`.
