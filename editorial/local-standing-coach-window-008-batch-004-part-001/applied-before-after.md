## D171-single-a — revised

Get someone to deliver the apology in the form, order, and setting you consider valid.

Mechanism: D171 makes A seek an apology whose delivery satisfies A's prescribed form, order, and setting.

Confusable concept: Seeking remorse alone would allow B2's sincere apology to settle the matter.

### before

**A:** Please apologise for eating my cake at the table where it happened.

**B:** I'm sorry. Does that help?

**A:** It's a promising rehearsal. The cake plate is over there.

**B:** There's no cake left.

**A:** Exactly. Let the setting do some of the work.

### after

**A:** Please apologise for eating my cake at the table where it happened.

**B:** I'm sorry. Does that help?

**A:** It's a promising rehearsal. The cake plate is over there.

**B:** Your dad's sitting there. Can't we do this privately?

**A:** He watched me blame the dog. Tell him you ate it before you tell me you're sorry.

First / second pass: 88 / 92

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 specifies the apology's setting; A3 treats an otherwise adequate apology as a rehearsal until delivered there. Result 5 — A1 requires the original table; A3 rejects an apology outside that setting; A5 specifies confession to Dad before apology to A.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 evaluates B2's apology; A5 makes B4's missing cake a reason to use the empty plate. Result 5 — B2's immediate apology becomes A3's 'rehearsal'; B4's objection to Dad's presence causes A5 to make Dad the first recipient.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 attempts reconciliation, while B4 questions the practical value of returning to the cake plate. Result 4 — B4 wants privacy, while A5 needs the witness who heard the dog blamed to receive a correction.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 turns B4's objection into a requirement that the absence of cake contribute to the apology. Result 4 — B4 makes the required location socially awkward; A5 increases the demand from a situated apology to a witnessed confession.
- specificity: original 5 — A1 immediately establishes an eaten cake, its owner, its eater, and the requested apology location. Result 5 — A1 establishes stolen cake and its table; A3 supplies the plate, and A5 gives Dad's presence a concrete purpose.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's 'promising rehearsal' economically reveals A's standards; A5 sustains that theatrical treatment. Result 4 — A3's 'promising rehearsal' economically invalidates B2's apology; A5's dog detail earns the required order.

B4 causes A5: B4 identifies Dad as the obstacle to privacy; A5 explains his relevance and requires confession to him first.

Alternatives: This alternative uses physical placement and embarrassment before a witness; D171-single-b uses written wording and control of the family account.

Reservation: A5 supplies the prior accusation late, although it credibly explains the setting requirement. No rewrite needed.
