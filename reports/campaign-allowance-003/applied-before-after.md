Mechanism: A makes a trivial favor travel through successive authorized levels even when the requested object is immediately accessible.

**B:** You're standing beside the teaspoons.

**A:** Then countersign your request. I'll pass it up to catering.

### after

**B:** You're standing beside the teaspoons.

**A:** Physical proximity is not authority.

**B:** I'm the kitchen supervisor.

**A:** Then countersign your request. I'll pass it up to catering.

First / second pass: 92 / 92

- relationship: original 4 — B2 wants a readily available teaspoon and B4 supplies the exact rank A1 required. Result 4 — B2 wants immediate access to nearby teaspoons and B4 supplies the rank A requested, forcing a new procedural response.
