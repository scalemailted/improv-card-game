## S67-single-b — revised

Treat every question, favor, and decision as something that must travel through the proper hierarchy.

Mechanism: A routes a neighbor's borrowing request through household superiors and treats acting authority as another step requiring confirmation.

Confusable concept: Power Vacuum: revised B4 identifies an acting head, yet A5 still forwards the request upward rather than merely resolving who is in charge.

### before

**A:** Before I lend you this ladder, your department head must ask mine.

**B:** We're neighbours, cleaning our gutters.

**A:** Then we'll need to establish departmental boundaries.

**B:** Can I just borrow it?

**A:** Not while the gutter committee remains unformed.

### after

**A:** Before I lend you this ladder, your department head must ask mine.

**B:** We're neighbours, cleaning gutters. My wife sent me over.

**A:** Then her request should go to my wife. I can supply the ladder's dimensions.

**B:** Your wife's away. She left you in charge.

**A:** Then address it to me as acting head. I'll forward it to her for confirmation.
