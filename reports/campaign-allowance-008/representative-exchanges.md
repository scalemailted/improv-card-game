## D99-single-b — Understand the Hurt

Make someone acknowledge why their action hurt, not merely agree that it happened.

**A:** I texted that I'd lost my job. You replied, 'What's for dinner?'

**B:** I thought something normal would help.

**A:** Making your dinner? Can you see why I felt like the cook?

**B:** I meant we could go out. I'll book somewhere.

**A:** I believe you. Before you book, can you see why I heard a chore? I needed you to ask how I was.

B4 → A5: B4 reveals that dinner meant going out and offers to book. A5 explicitly accepts the revelation with 'I believe you' and delays that booking until B understands why the original message hurt.

Saved fresh-review total: 89/100. Reservation: A5 repeats the domestic-chore interpretation from A3, limiting heightening. The added emotional need and direct response to booking make the original acceptable without a rewrite. This is an internal second-pass assessment.

## D102-single-b — What Aren’t You Saying?

Find out what someone is holding back by treating every careful answer as incomplete.

**A:** These minutes say 'discussion of my proposal.' What did you leave out?

**B:** Sign them and we can eat. Nobody wanted an awkward record.

**A:** 'Nobody' sounds crowded. Who wanted my name out of it?

**B:** I did. I suggested someone else present, to save your proposal.

**A:** Who else? You've recorded my rescue very carefully without naming my replacement.

B4 → A5: B4 admits suggesting another presenter; A5 now demands that person's name and challenges the protective framing.

Saved fresh-review total: 88/100. Reservation: A3 assumes deliberate removal of A's name before B has established it. This is playable suspicion, but a small inferential jump. No rewrite needed.

## D105-single-b — See What This Took

Make someone recognize the courage, restraint, or emotional labor your choices required.

**A:** I told the committee I couldn't chair another year. Please appreciate what those two sentences took.

**B:** Good. Could you stay on until we find someone?

**A:** I rehearsed in your car until I stopped apologising. That wasn't me advertising a temporary vacancy.

**B:** Then I'll tell them you're exhausted and need a break.

**A:** Please don't supply the excuse I spent an hour removing. Could you recognise a refusal I finally managed without one?

B4 → A5: B4 proposes explaining the departure through exhaustion; A5 identifies that explanation as the very excuse A worked to eliminate and requests recognition of that achievement.

Saved fresh-review total: 92/100. Reservation: A1 states the recognition request rather explicitly, and A5 is more emotionally precise than surprising. The sustained card behavior and causal exchange justify acceptance without a rewrite.
