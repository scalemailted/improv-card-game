## S132-single-a — revised

Mechanism: A treats a present exchange as unfinished until a specific unresolved concealment from the past is addressed.

Confusable concept: Holding a grudge can seek apology or punishment without needing the missing information that A demands here.

**A:** And every new itinerary still has that blank.

**B:** Can't we start fresh?

**A:** Yes. Answer the first question so it stops packing with us.

### after

**A:** Before we book our anniversary trip, why did you leave Venice early?

**B:** I'll stay the whole week this time. Nonrefundable.

**A:** You paid for the last night then, too. What happened?

**B:** Mum was ill. She asked me not to tell anyone.

**A:** You could have said that afterwards. When you said you needed space from me, was that her request too?

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 blocks the new holiday with the old departure; A5 makes answering that question the condition for starting fresh. Result 5 — A1 makes booking an anniversary trip conditional on explaining Venice; A3 rejects financial reassurance; A5 probes the broken confidence left unresolved by B4.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 counters B2's elapsed years with continuing itineraries; A5 directly conditions B4's proposed fresh start. Result 5 — B2 offers a nonrefundable commitment, which A3 counters with the previously paid last night. B4 supplies Mum's secrecy request, which A5 tests against the explanation previously given to A.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 minimizes the old event and B4 wants a renewed shared holiday, giving A a credible avoidance to confront. Result 4 — B2 wants another trip and offers reassurance; B4 reveals a competing family confidence. A5 exposes how that confidence affected the couple, though B has little room to elaborate.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 turns B4's fresh-start proposal into a requirement that the old question be answered first. Result 4 — B4 apparently answers the departure question, but A5 identifies a deeper injury: being told the partner needed space. The inquiry moves from absence to damaged trust.

- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's itinerary blank and A5's question 'packing with us' extend the holiday situation economically. Result 3 — A3 economically turns B2's payment reassurance against it. A5 earns its emotional turn, but the exchange offers more relationship drama than comic surprise.

B4 causes A5: B4 attributes concealment to Mum's request; A5 asks whether that request also explains the hurtful account given to A and the continued silence afterwards.

Alternatives: This alternative uses intimate hurt and tests a partner's explanation. The preserved club alternative uses membership leverage and an offered institutional role to pursue an erased vote; its tactic and relationship are meaningfully different.

Reservation: The final disclosure is coherent but introduces consequential relationship history late. The scene plays more readily as sincere drama than comedy. No rewrite needed for this internal pass.
