## S101-single-a — revised

Use your experience with hardship as emotional seniority, offering guidance and judging how well others are handling the pressure.

Mechanism: A converts having endured hardship first into authority to prescribe coping and grade B's reactions.

Confusable concept: S106 competes for greater sympathy through greater pain; S101 claims superior qualifications to guide someone else.

### before

**A:** My first failed opening was worse. Tea before reading the reviews.

**B:** I'd rather read them now.

**A:** That's a beginner's relationship with disappointment.

**B:** Maybe I handle it differently.

**A:** Possibly. Try my way before inventing suffering.

### after

**A:** Your first restaurant review? When mine came, I made tea before opening it.

**B:** I've opened it. Two stars. I'm ringing the critic.

**A:** That's the stage where I unplugged my phone. Milk?

**B:** I've already dialled.

**A:** Then let it ring while the tea brews. You're rushing a mistake I took all afternoon over.

First / second pass: 81 / 92

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 claims prior hardship; A3 judges B a beginner; A5 prescribes A's coping method. Result 5 — A1 establishes prior review hardship; A3 prescribes A's coping method; A5 judges B's speed against A's own mistake.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 judges B2's immediate reading, but A5 answers B4's difference with a largely repeatable command. Result 5 — B2's proposed call produces A3's unplugged-phone advice; B4's already-dialled call forces A5 to recommend delaying the answer.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 wants immediate information and B4 asserts an individual way of coping. Result 4 — B2 wants to confront the critic and B4 acts before A can prevent it, giving the experienced colleague a credible obstacle.
- heightening: original 3 — A5 intensifies the condescension after B4 without materially developing the tea prescription. Result 4 — A5 extends tea-making into control of an active call and claims seniority even in making the same mistake.
- specificity: original 3 — A1's 'failed opening' and reviews suggest a performance but leave the occasion indistinct. Result 5 — A1's first restaurant review, B2's two stars and A3's offer of milk establish an immediately playable encounter.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's 'beginner's relationship' earns its condescension; A5's 'inventing suffering' is less concrete. Result 4 — A3's 'Milk?' sustains domestic composure; A5's 'all afternoon' earns its joke through A's competitive experience.

B4 causes A5: B4 makes prevention too late by revealing the call is dialled; A5 switches to letting it ring while tea brews.

Alternatives: Unlike the redundancy alternative's dismissal of good news, this scene gives A an unfolding risky action to manage through a personal coping ritual.

Reservation: A5's permission to let the call ring offers only a short practical delay, but the seniority claim remains clear. No rewrite needed.
