| S39 | Emotional Assumptions | Fear & Insecurity | **Don’t Be Difficult** | Treat every request, correction, or small inconvenience as a warning that you are becoming too much trouble. |

| S47 | Worldview & Absurdity | Fixation & Significance | **One Tiny Imperfection** | Choose one tiny imperfection and let it steadily outweigh every larger concern. |
| S48 | Worldview & Absurdity | Pattern & Grand Meaning | **Nothing Is Ever Simple** | Interpret each new complication as proof that ordinary tasks are secretly designed to become impossible. |
