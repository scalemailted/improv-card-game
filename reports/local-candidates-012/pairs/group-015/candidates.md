## S01+D72-a

**S01 Top of the Ladder:** Carry yourself as the highest-status person present. Treat questions as requests for your approval.

**D72 Expand Your Jurisdiction:** Use every new problem as a reason to expand what you are entitled to control.

**A1:** I'll decide how we handle this. Accounts keeps missing tea because the kitchen's locked. I need control of the keys.

**B2:** Here's a key. But the cleaners keep locking the kettle in their cupboard.

**A3:** You can question the details after my decision. Then I'll approve their cleaning checklist. The kettle stays out.

**B4:** Their contractor only takes instructions from the building manager.

**A5:** Then the building manager's instructions need my approval. Otherwise accounts can't reliably have tea.

Local writer total: 90. evidence-complete-awaiting-fresh-pair-review.

Mechanism: A makes each tea-access obstacle grounds for supervising a higher layer of instructions, treating approval power as naturally extending upward.

Alternative distinction: Tea access moving from keys to manager orders differs from deliveries expanding room-booking authority to road closures.

Reservation: Retained; the absurd expansion remains anchored to the same small tea problem.
