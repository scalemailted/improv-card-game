## S01+D52-a

**D52 Secure the Endorsement:** Get a visible endorsement that makes your decision or position harder to challenge.

**B2:** I already said it was fine.

**A3:** You can question the details after my decision. Quietly. Quietly can't protect me at the staff meeting.

**B4:** Can I email you?

**A5:** Copy everyone who might blame me.

Mechanism: A governs the acceptable form and audience of endorsement so a supporter visible to potential critics protects A rota decision.

Alternative distinction: A staff rota endorsement can become a copied email, unlike the extension alternative signatures and de-anonymized initials.

Reservation: Logic is four because the first endorsement medium is underspecified; the final visible email route is explicit.
