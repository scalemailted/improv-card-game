## S231-single-a — revised

Treat every reassuring development as evidence that the real threat has merely changed shape.

Mechanism: A treats reassuring inspection and containment results as evidence that the restaurant's threat has migrated and become concentrated.

Confusable concept: General pessimism anticipates failure; this card turns successful cleaning and secure containment into affirmative evidence of a transformed threat.

### before

**A:** The inspection passed? That's suspiciously convenient.

**B:** They found nothing wrong.

**A:** Then the problem has become good at hiding.

**B:** They checked everything twice.

**A:** Whatever escaped that deserves a more serious inspection.

### after

**A:** Our restaurant passed inspection? Even the grease trap?

**B:** Spotless. I had it pumped out. Can I start cooking?

**A:** So the trouble's moved into the tanker. Is it still outside?

**B:** It's sealed. Nothing can escape.

**A:** Then we've bottled it under pressure beside our diners. Ask the driver to leave before I unlock the doors.

First / second pass: 85 / 88

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A3 makes a clean inspection evidence of concealment; A5 treats thoroughness as proof of greater danger. Result 5 — B2's cleaned grease trap becomes relocated trouble in A3; B4's sealed tanker becomes concentrated danger in A5. Both reassurances change the threat's shape.
- responseLogic: original 5 — B2's 'nothing wrong' produces A3's hiding theory; B4's double check produces A5's escalation. Result 4 — A3 takes the tanker specifically from B2's pumping; A5 converts B4's seal into pressure. The pressure inference is suspicious character logic, though physically unestablished.
- relationship: original 3 — B2 and B4 defend the inspection, but B has no concrete stake in accepting its result. Result 4 — B2 wants permission to start cooking; A5 makes opening the restaurant conditional on removing the tanker, giving A's suspicion a workplace consequence.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 demands a more serious inspection because B4 establishes that ordinary checking was thorough. Result 4 — A3 asks where the displaced trouble is; after B4, A5 treats containment as a danger to diners and delays opening.
- specificity: original 3 — A1 establishes an inspection but never identifies the inspected place, object, or practical decision. Result 5 — A1 establishes a restaurant inspection and grease trap; B2's cooking request and A5's locked doors make the immediate task playable.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's problem becoming 'good at hiding' economically converts reassurance into threat. Result 4 — A3's trouble moving into the tanker and A5's 'bottled it under pressure' express one economical suspicious logic.

B4 causes A5: B4 offers a sealed container as reassurance; A5 interprets that seal as dangerous pressure and requires its removal.

Alternatives: The preserved alternative turns committee acceptance and personal support into surveillance. This candidate uses operational reassurance and A's authority over opening a restaurant.

Reservation: The tanker being under pressure is A's inference rather than an established fact. It remains readable as sincere suspicion. No rewrite needed.
