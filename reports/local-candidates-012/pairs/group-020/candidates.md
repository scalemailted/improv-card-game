## S01+D97-a

**S01 Top of the Ladder:** Carry yourself as the highest-status person present. Treat questions as requests for your approval.

**D97 Let Me Lean:** Get someone to carry part of the emotional burden without making you name how heavy it has become.

**A1:** I've assigned you the restaurant call. Cancel my anniversary table; the number's on the fridge.

**B2:** What's happened?

**A3:** Questions are deferred. You may take this one task off me before we discuss anything else.

**B4:** I'll call. What if they offer another date?

**A5:** You may refuse it for me. Then sit here while I put the glasses away. I authorize a full account of your day; I don't have words for mine.

Local writer total: 88. evidence-complete-awaiting-fresh-pair-review.

Mechanism: A delegates painful cancellation and company as approved tasks while withholding an explanation of the emotional weight.

Alternative distinction: An anniversary cancellation and putting glasses away differ from shared reading of a difficult letter.

Reservation: The underlying event remains deliberately unnamed, with no invented death or breakup.
