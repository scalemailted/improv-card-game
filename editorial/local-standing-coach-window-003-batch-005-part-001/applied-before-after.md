## S171-single-a — revised

Treat forms, standards, and exact wording as a technical craft. Correct shortcuts before discussing whether the result actually works.

Mechanism: S171 makes A practice exact compliance as a craft and postpone judging practical success until the technical discrepancy is corrected.

Confusable concept: Generic pedantry or obstruction can reject a result without undertaking any concrete correction.

### before

**A:** Your evacuation plan uses the wrong shade of red.

**B:** It got everyone safely outside.

**A:** Without compliant arrows, that's technically a gathering.

**B:** The building was on fire.

**A:** Then the arrows should have been particularly unambiguous.

### after

**A:** Before we sign off your evacuation plan, these arrows need the approved red.

**B:** Everyone found the exit during the drill.

**A:** Good. We'll discuss that after the arrows match the sample.

**B:** The sample faded in the window.

**A:** Then we need a replacement sample before we can establish what everyone successfully followed.

First / second pass: 72 / 92

- cardFidelity: original 4 — A1 corrects the red; A3 puts compliant arrows ahead of B2's successful evacuation, but invents a dubious technical classification. Result 5 — A3 explicitly postpones discussing the successful drill until the arrows match; A5 subjects the reference sample itself to technical correction.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 disputes B2's successful result; A5 uses B4's fire, though it merely reinforces the original correction. Result 5 — B2's successful evacuation becomes A3's deferred discussion; B4's faded sample makes A5 demand a replacement standard.
- relationship: original 3 — B2 defends getting everyone outside and B4 names the emergency, but neither changes A's available corrective task. Result 4 — B2 defends practical success and B4 challenges the inspection standard, obliging A to change the prerequisite for approval.
- heightening: original 3 — A5's 'particularly unambiguous' intensifies emphasis without adding a concrete consequence to A1's objection. Result 4 — A5 expands A1's arrow correction into replacing the authority used to judge the arrows, despite B2's established success.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 supplies an evacuation plan and a color error; B4 clarifies the practical stakes. Result 5 — A1 establishes evacuation-plan signoff, approved red arrows and an inspector relationship in one playable sentence.
- brevityWit: original 3 — A3's 'technically a gathering' supplies the main joke, but sounds more like a quip than technical craft. Result 4 — A5's 'what everyone successfully followed' earns its absurdity from B2, although the sentence is slightly formal.

B4 causes A5: B4 makes the sample unreliable, so A5 requires a replacement before assessing the successful drill.

Alternatives: Compared with S171-single-b's greeting inspection, this alternative has stronger physical logic and a credible institutional approval relationship.

Reservation: A5 is somewhat polished and formal, but the causal escalation is clear. No rewrite needed; the supplied dialogue is retained.
