## S118-single-a — revised

Accept every feeling as real, then translate it into water, space, a plan, a boundary, or the next practical step.

Mechanism: A accepts the feeling without contesting it and converts each stated need into a manageable practical accommodation.

Confusable concept: Managing another person's emotions imposes a preferred state; A accepts continued upset and follows B's expressed boundary.

### before

**A:** You're upset. Water, quiet, or a walk?

**B:** Water won't fix this.

**A:** No. It makes being upset less thirsty.

**B:** I don't want to explain.

**A:** Then quiet comes with the water. No questionnaire.

### after

**A:** Opening night without the lead you wanted hurts. Water, or some space in my dressing room?

**B:** Water won't make me happy for her.

**A:** No. It just means you can be furious without a dry throat.

**B:** She's coming to ask if I'm okay.

**A:** I'll ask her to wait until after the show. You only owe her your cues tonight.

- responseLogic: original 5 — A3 agrees with B2 that water cannot fix the feeling; A5 directly honors B4's refusal to explain. Result 5 — A3 answers B2's objection that water cannot create happiness; A5 responds directly to B4's approaching lead with a delay and a cues-only obligation.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 rejects a cure and B4 sets a disclosure boundary, each changing the form of A's support. Result 5 — B2 resists pressure to feel generous and B4 reveals imminent social pressure; A3 and A5 respect both concerns while providing concrete support.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 adds protection from questioning to the original water offer rather than merely repeating it. Result 4 — A5 moves from bodily comfort to managing another performer's access and defining what B owes during the show.
- specificity: original 3 — A1 supplies immediate options and distress, although neither the relationship nor the cause is established. Result 4 — A1 supplies opening night and a dressing room, with A5's cues confirming fellow performers; 'without the lead you wanted' is briefly ambiguous.
- brevityWit: original 5 — A3's 'less thirsty' precisely limits the offer; A5's 'No questionnaire' economically enacts the boundary. Result 4 — A3's fury without a dry throat expresses the card economically; A5's 'only owe her your cues' makes the boundary specific to their work.

Reservation: A1's 'lead you wanted' can initially suggest a preferred lead performer rather than a desired role. Later turns resolve the intended situation. No rewrite needed.

## S118-single-b — revised

Accept every feeling as real, then translate it into water, space, a plan, a boundary, or the next practical step.

Mechanism: A recognizes anger as valid and adapts food, timing, and conversational boundaries to make the next task manageable.

Confusable concept: Distracting someone out of anger aims to change the feeling; A arranges the journey so anger can remain.

### before

**A:** We can be furious and still eat something. Toast?

**B:** I can't think about food.

**A:** I'll put it here so thinking isn't required.

**B:** Don't tell me to calm down.

**A:** I won't. Furious toast is still toast.

### after

**A:** You can be furious about the cancelled train and still have toast before we drive.

**B:** I can't think about food.

**A:** I'll wrap it. You can decide when we're on the road.

**B:** Don't spend the drive telling me to calm down.

**A:** Agreed. Directions only. The toast won't require a better attitude either.

First / second pass: 78 / 95

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 permits fury alongside eating and A3 removes the need to decide about food. Result 5 — A1 allows fury alongside toast and travel; A3 makes eating optional; A5 accepts a boundary without requiring an improved mood.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 uses B2's inability to think about food; A5 honors B4 verbally but adds no new practical accommodation. Result 5 — B2's inability to think about food makes A3 wrap it for later; B4's objection to calming advice makes A5 promise directions only.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 identifies limited capacity and B4 resists being told to calm down, constraining A's help. Result 5 — B2 controls whether to eat and B4 sets terms for shared travel; A3 and A5 adapt useful assistance to both choices.
- heightening: original 2 — A5's furious toast restates A1's coexistence of anger and food without developing the support. Result 4 — A5 extends practical acceptance from a snack to the conduct of the entire drive, prompted by B4's anticipated pressure.
- specificity: original 3 — A1 supplies toast and fury but no event or immediate task around them. Result 5 — A1 establishes a cancelled train, anger, toast and a replacement drive, giving the exchange a clear immediate task.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A5's toast phrase is compact and relevant, though it substitutes a slogan for further action. Result 4 — A5's 'Directions only' states the practical boundary crisply; the toast's lack of attitude requirements follows A1 and A3.

B4 causes A5: B4 refuses calming commentary during the drive; A5 limits speech to directions and removes emotional conditions from the food.

Alternatives: Alternative b gives cooperative care an external problem and future plan; a remains an abstract exchange about upset and water.

Reservation: No rewrite needed. The final toast personification is slightly polished but remains tied to the concrete support.
