## D151-single-a — revised

Recruit someone into treating your impossible explanation as the most useful version of events.

Mechanism: D151 recruits B to use an impossible explanation because it offers a workable account of what to do next.

Confusable concept: D155 requests one premise-dependent choice; D151 seeks shared use of an explanatory framework.

### before

**A:** The house is migrating. Help me choose where it should rest.

**B:** Houses don't migrate.

**A:** This one packed its chimney. We should support its journey.

**B:** Where could it go?

**A:** Somewhere with good foundations and a watering hole.

### after

**A:** The house is migrating south. Help me redirect it before we lose the driveway.

**B:** The surveyor says subsidence. He's bringing concrete.

**A:** Concrete traps it halfway across the road. Tell him we're planning a route.

**B:** His truck's blocking the road already.

**A:** Perfect. Ask him to lead slowly toward the empty plot. It needs a guide.

First / second pass: 54 / 93

- cardFidelity: original 3 — A1 recruits help choosing a destination, but A3 asks for support without making migration a useful explanation. Result 5 — A3 turns B2's concrete repair into an obstacle to migration; A5 recruits B to arrange a guide for the house.
- responseLogic: original 3 — A3 counters B2 with a packed chimney; A5 answers B4 but adds an unsupported watering hole. Result 5 — B2 supplies concrete, which A3 says would trap the house; B4 supplies the truck, which A5 repurposes as its guide.
- relationship: original 2 — B2 disputes migration and B4 asks where; neither gives B a practical stake. Result 4 — B2 brings the surveyor's ordinary repair plan, and B4 identifies an immediate obstruction that changes A's tactic.
- heightening: original 2 — A5 supplies a destination preference without increasing A1's commitment or consequence. Result 5 — A5 converts B4's blocked road into a plan to enlist the surveyor and relocate the house to an empty plot.
- specificity: original 3 — A1 identifies a migrating house, but no immediate household problem explains the request. Result 4 — A1 establishes a threatened driveway; B2's surveyor and B4's truck give the exchange concrete, playable objects.
- brevityWit: original 3 — A3's packed chimney is economical, while A5's watering hole adds animal imagery rather than useful logic. Result 4 — A5's 'It needs a guide' economically completes the conversion of B4's obstruction into migration assistance.

B4 causes A5: B4 puts the surveyor's truck in the road; A5 makes that truck the house's guide.

Alternatives: Unlike D151-single-b's workplace negotiation and B's helpful offer, this alternative converts an ordinary repair plan and obstruction into migration logistics.

Reservation: The house's ability to follow a truck is an additional assumption, though consistent with A's sincere migration premise. No rewrite needed.
