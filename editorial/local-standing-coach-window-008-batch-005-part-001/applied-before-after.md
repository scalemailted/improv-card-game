## D181-single-a — revised

Hide your role in the problem by distributing blame across steps, policies, and unnamed procedures.

Mechanism: D181 conceals A's personal contribution by spreading responsibility among procedural stages.

Confusable concept: Generic jargon disguises words; this card specifically disperses responsibility for A's own mistake.

### before

**A:** Your parcel encountered a breakdown between reception and dispatch.

**B:** You left it on the bus.

**A:** That was the transport interface.

**B:** You were going home.

**A:** Yes. A regrettable overlap between staffing and distribution.

### after

**A:** Your parcel got caught between reception and dispatch.

**B:** You left it on the bus.

**A:** Dispatch handed it to transport without a receipt.

**B:** Both departments are you. Call the bus company.

**A:** Certainly. I'll report a failure across dispatch, transport and receipt control. They'll need to investigate all three.

First / second pass: 81 / 90

- cardFidelity: original 4 — A1 distributes responsibility between reception and dispatch; A3 renames the bus a transport interface. Result 5 — A3 converts leaving the parcel on a bus into an undocumented dispatch transfer; A5 spreads responsibility across three functions.
- responseLogic: original 5 — A3 specifically absorbs B2's bus; A5 translates B4's journey home into overlapping functions. Result 5 — B2 supplies the bus, which becomes transport in A3; B4 requests a call, which A5 turns into a report demanding a wider investigation.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 and B4 insist on A's personal responsibility for B's parcel. Result 4 — B2 identifies A's actual mistake, and B4 wants practical recovery through calling the bus company.
- heightening: original 3 — A5 adds staffing and distribution but largely repeats A3's euphemistic substitution. Result 4 — After B4 collapses the departments back into A, A5 adds receipt control and recruits an investigation into the evasion.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 establishes a missing parcel; B2 locates the actual mistake on a bus. Result 4 — A1 establishes a missing parcel and its owner; B2 makes the concrete loss immediately playable.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's short 'transport interface' neatly bureaucratizes B2's concrete accusation. Result 4 — A3's 'without a receipt' earns A5's receipt-control department, although A5 explains its escalation at some length.

B4 causes A5: B4 orders the bus-company call; A5 agrees while converting that call into an investigation of several processes.

Alternatives: Unlike the birthday alternative's reclassification of personal fault as understaffing, this version appropriates B's practical recovery proposal.

Reservation: A5 is slightly explanatory, and its departmental language is conspicuously elaborate for a personal parcel. No rewrite needed.
