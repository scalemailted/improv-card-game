## S01+D122-a

**S01 Top of the Ladder:** Carry yourself as the highest-status person present. Treat questions as requests for your approval.

**D122 Not Until You Explain:** Keep someone present until they account for one contradiction, omission, or suspicious change in their story.

**A1:** I'll decide how we handle this. Before you catch that bus: you said work kept you till six. Why's your taxi receipt timed five?

**B2:** I'll explain tomorrow. That's the last bus.

**A3:** You can question the details after my decision. I'll drive you. What happened to that hour?

**B4:** I left at five. I stopped somewhere before I came home.

**A5:** I'll drive you home after you tell me where you stopped. Sit down; I've got all evening.

Local writer total: 86. evidence-complete-awaiting-fresh-pair-review.

Mechanism: A uses command of transport and departure to keep B present until the suspicious missing hour is explained.

Alternative distinction: A taxi time and ride offer differ from a wedding booking and work departure.

Reservation: A's control may feel coercive, but the character tactic is explicit; do not invent a legal power to detain.
