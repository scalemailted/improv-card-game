# Imprompt v0.17.0 — Advanced Scene Engines

**Release type:** Playtest content expansion  
**Pack:** 10 of 10  
**Reserved IDs:** S217–S240 and D217–D240

Imprompt v0.17.0 adds **Pack 10: Advanced Scene Engines**, expanding the active library from 432 to the complete planned **480 prompts** while preserving independent decks, Guided Exercises, Scene Log snapshots, Practice Coverage, gallery tools, QR sharing, and offline operation.

## Library completion

The active library now contains:

- 240 Stances;
- 240 Drives;
- 480 total cards;
- all 10 planned packs active;
- 1 published pack and 9 playtest packs.

Advanced Scene Engines contributes one new card to every formal Card Bible subtheme: 24 Stances and 24 Drives distributed across the established seven categories.

## Editorial focus

The pack makes the following veteran-level skills playable without imposing a scripted plot:

- callbacks whose meaning changes;
- delayed reveals that recontextualize instead of invalidate;
- simultaneous and contradictory pressures;
- earned reversals of status, meaning, allegiance, or objective;
- recurring details and relationship patterns;
- foreshadowing built from existing offers;
- practical and emotional consequences that make earlier choices matter;
- structural listening across the full scene.

The complete authoring package contains a 64-card candidate pool: 48 selected, 8 held, and 8 rejected with rationale.

## Guided Exercise impact

Existing exercise configurations automatically draw from the enlarged library. The pack is especially useful for advanced versions of Status Clash, Crown & Heart, Past & Future, Pursuer & Avoider, Absurd Commitment, Instigator & Anchor, and custom exercises centered on callbacks, subtext, double binds, foreshadowing, or reversal.

Every phone still shuffles independently. Exercise links and QR codes share only configuration and optional role assignment.

## Migration

Opening v0.17.0 with a valid earlier deck preserves the current scene, sessions, Scene Log, immutable historical wording, consumed cards, remaining queue order, Practice Coverage, and saved coach exercises. Only S217–S240 and D217–D240 are inserted into the remaining local queues.

## Offline and cache behavior

The service-worker cache and versioned asset URLs advance to `imprompt-v0.17.0`. The stable service-worker address and non-forced activation strategy remain unchanged. The new pack module is precached for offline play.
