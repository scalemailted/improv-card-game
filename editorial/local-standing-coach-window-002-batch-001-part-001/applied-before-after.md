## S81-single-a — revised

Return immediately to the role you usually play in this relationship, even when the present situation calls for something different.

Mechanism: S81 makes A immediately resume an established relationship role despite a present need for different behavior.

Confusable concept: General compulsive helpfulness can look similar without any evidence that fixing is this relationship's familiar division of labor.

### before

**A:** You're upset. I'll make tea and telephone whoever needs sorting out.

**B:** Could you just listen?

**A:** Of course. Who am I listening about?

**B:** Nobody needs fixing.

**A:** Then I'll fix the temperature of the tea while you explain.

### after

**A:** You're crying, love. I'll make tea and ring your manager, like last time.

**B:** Mum, I've been promoted. Could you just listen?

**A:** Of course. Will they give you enough staff?

**B:** Yes. Just raise your cup and say congratulations.

**A:** Congratulations. Now, who covers you when you're ill?

First / second pass: 68 / 95

- cardFidelity: original 3 — A1 offers practical rescue and A5 returns to fixing, but neither establishes this as A's usual relationship role. Result 5 — A1 establishes habitual maternal crisis management with 'like last time'; A3 and A5 immediately return to protective questioning despite celebration being requested.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 answers B2's listening request; A5 redirects fixing toward tea after B4 excludes fixing people. Result 5 — B2's promotion produces A3's staffing concern; B4's requested congratulations produces A5's brief compliance followed by concern about sickness cover.
- relationship: original 3 — B2 wants listening and B4 protects that wish, but A1 leaves their shared history unspecified. Result 5 — B2 identifies Mum and asks to be heard; B4 gives her a manageable celebratory role that A5 converts back into mothering.
- heightening: original 3 — A5 preserves the fixing impulse through tea, though it reduces rather than substantially extends A1's intervention. Result 4 — A5 absorbs B4's explicit toast instruction and resumes oversight immediately, although sickness cover is only a modest escalation from A3's staffing question.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 supplies upset, tea and a possible telephone call; B2 gives an immediately playable emotional need. Result 5 — A1's tears, tea and previous manager intervention establish playable mistaken concern; B2 clearly reveals the actual occasion.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A5's tea-temperature workaround earns its joke from B4's refusal of repair. Result 4 — A5's 'Congratulations. Now' economically exposes the reflex, though the two employment questions in A3 and A5 are closely similar.

B4 causes A5: B4 specifies a toast; A5 delivers it before immediately resuming protective management.

Alternatives: Alternative b uses a sibling's covert excuse-making; this version uses a mother's overt protective questions during good news.

Reservation: The final concern varies the staffing concern more than it increases the stakes. No rewrite needed.
