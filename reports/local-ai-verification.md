labeled test doubles. They are not evidence of model quality or actual ONNX loading.

A library source review caught and corrected a configuration incompatibility:
Transformers.js 3.8.1 requires allowLocalModels=true when local_files_only=true.
The adapter enables that flag, disables remote models, and supplies pinned cached
responses through its custom cache. The worker tests lock in this combination.

A DOM check caught and corrected unequal-height desktop card surfaces: surfaces
now stretch to the grid cell, keeping their sibling absolute buttons inside the
same boundary. The old negative margin above the combo button was also removed.

## Not established by these checks

- Download/CORS success on the deployed origin.
- Actual Transformers.js Blob import, ONNX model initialization, WASM execution.
- Quality of sampled FLAN-T5-small output for any card pair.
- Actual generation latency or peak RAM on Pixel, iPhone, or desktop hardware.
- Real service-worker/model offline reload after install.
- Human blind-read, live-scene success, or suitability of all combinations.

The inherited exhaustive 57,600-hand / 345,600-angle audit is for the finite
**deterministic** hint engine. It is not a neural-generation benchmark.
