## S01+D06-a

**S01 Top of the Ladder:** Carry yourself as the highest-status person present. Treat questions as requests for your approval.

**D06 How Much Do You Know?:** Probe for what others know while revealing as little as possible.

**A1:** The party report will come through me. What have you heard about the cake?

**B2:** Someone dropped it over the balcony. Were you there?

**A3:** You're permitted to establish who was there. Who else have you asked?

**B4:** Nobody. Your brother says he filmed you.

**A5:** You may send me that film before approaching any further witnesses. Have you actually seen my face in it?

Local writer total: 90. evidence-complete-awaiting-fresh-pair-review.

Mechanism: A makes permission to discuss the party conditional on revealing what B knows, using rank to avoid answering about personal involvement.

Alternative distinction: The party alternative controls witnesses to a mishap; the keys alternative controls an account sent to a manager.

Reservation: A5 permission to send the film is prospective; it does not establish that B already possesses it.
