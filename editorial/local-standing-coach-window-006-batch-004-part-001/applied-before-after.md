## D71-single-a — revised

Treat every partial agreement as confirmation that everyone has accepted your leadership.

Mechanism: D71 converts limited agreement into evidence that everyone accepts A's leadership.

Confusable concept: This can resemble exaggerating support for a proposal, which need not imply personal leadership.

### before

**A:** You nodded at Friday. I'll announce unanimous support for my plan.

**B:** I agreed to Friday, not the plan.

**A:** Excellent. We've approved the timetable of my leadership.

**B:** I object to the budget.

**A:** Then I'll announce unanimous calendar support first.

### after

**A:** You nodded at Friday. I'll tell the committee you've accepted me as project lead.

**B:** I only agreed to the meeting date.

**A:** Exactly. Everyone's following the timetable I set.

**B:** I'll attend, but I'm voting against your budget.

**A:** Then I'll introduce you as my finance watchdog. It's good to have the whole committee behind my leadership.

First / second pass: 61 / 90

- cardFidelity: original 3 — A3 claims approval of 'my leadership,' but A5 retreats to calendar support instead of treating it as acceptance of leadership. Result 5 — A1 converts agreement on Friday into acceptance of leadership; A5 converts attendance despite opposition into committee backing.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 uses B2's Friday distinction; A5 avoids B4's budget objection by returning to the calendar. Result 5 — B2 limits agreement to the date, which A3 calls following A's timetable; B4's budget opposition becomes A5's finance-watchdog appointment.
- relationship: original 3 — B2 limits consent and B4 objects to spending, but neither establishes B's practical stake in the announcement. Result 4 — B2 corrects A's claim and B4 preserves an opposing vote while agreeing to attend, giving B a credible committee position.
- heightening: original 2 — After B4 objects to the budget, A5 reduces the claim to 'calendar support' rather than increasing leadership entitlement. Result 4 — A5 goes beyond A1's claimed endorsement by assigning the budget opponent an office within A's presumed leadership.
- specificity: original 3 — A1 supplies Friday and a planned announcement, but 'my plan' leaves the shared undertaking indistinct. Result 4 — A1 supplies Friday, a committee and a project-lead claim; B4 adds a concrete budget dispute, though the project remains unspecified.
- brevityWit: original 3 — A3's 'timetable of my leadership' is compact but sounds more composed than sincerely managerial. Result 4 — A3's timetable argument is economical; A5's 'finance watchdog' earns the turn, although its final sentence restates the premise.

B4 causes A5: B4 agrees to attend but opposes the budget; A5 assimilates both into a finance role under A.

Alternatives: Alternative a absorbs resistance through an assigned oversight role; b appropriates a cooperative proposal as delegated authority.

Reservation: A5's final claim of whole-committee support explains a joke already conveyed by the appointment. No rewrite needed.
