## D101-single-a — retained

Get someone else to explain, announce, or begin the emotionally difficult part.

Mechanism: A recruits B to deliver the emotionally difficult opening while reserving a less exposed supporting job.

Confusable concept: Avoiding all responsibility is the closest confusion; this drive specifically delegates the difficult beginning.

### before

**A:** You tell the choir the tour's cancelled. I'll hand out the refund forms.

**B:** You're the organiser.

**A:** Exactly. The forms will need my full attention.

**B:** They'll ask why.

**A:** Say 'funding' first. I'll join after the disappointed silence.

### after

**A:** You tell the choir the tour's cancelled. I'll hand out the refund forms.

**B:** You're the organiser.

**A:** Exactly. The forms will need my full attention.

**B:** They'll ask why.

**A:** Say 'funding' first. I'll join after the disappointed silence.

First / second pass: 92 / 90

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 delegates the cancellation; A5 makes B say 'funding' and absorb the disappointed silence. Result 5 — A1 delegates the cancellation announcement; A5 explicitly waits until after the disappointed silence.
- responseLogic: original 5 — A3 turns B2's organiser objection into paperwork duty; A5 supplies the explanation B4 anticipates. Result 4 — A3 turns B2's organiser responsibility into paperwork cover; A5 answers B4's anticipated why with 'funding'.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 holds the organiser responsible, and B4 raises the choir's practical need for an explanation. Result 4 — B2 challenges the organiser's delegation, and B4 identifies the explanation the choir will demand.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 extends A1's delegated announcement to its explanation and emotional aftermath. Result 4 — A5 narrows A1's delegated announcement into a precise entry point after B absorbs disappointment.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 establishes a cancelled choir tour and refund forms, although B's exact role remains open. Result 5 — A1 supplies choir, cancelled tour and refund forms in an immediately playable request.
- brevityWit: original 5 — A3's 'full attention' and A5's delayed entrance both earn their humour through sincere avoidance. Result 5 — A3's 'full attention' and A5's 'disappointed silence' make concise, character-driven evasions.

B4 causes A5: B4 predicts a request for reasons; A5 supplies the opening explanation and delegates its emotional reception.

Alternatives: Alternative a uses public responsibility and paperwork; b bargains over participation in a family conversation.

Reservation: A3 uses a familiar administrative excuse, but the causal exchange is clear. Retain unchanged.
