## D126-single-a — revised

Find the truth by comparing details, repeating questions, and testing which parts of the story remain consistent.

Mechanism: A seeks truth by comparing inconsistent labels with observable details and repeating the account to test a possible reconciliation.

Confusable concept: Competing Theories requires shifting allegiance among explanations; D126 can investigate consistency without choosing a favored theory.

### before

**A:** Was the parcel in a bag or a box? You've used both words.

**B:** A bag, I think.

**A:** Describe the handles before choosing the noun.

**B:** It had a rigid lid.

**A:** Good. Let's build the object before the story.

### after

**A:** For this missing-parcel form: was it a bag or a box? You've said both.

**B:** A bag, I think. I carried it by the handles.

**A:** Describe the handles. Were they attached to fabric or cardboard?

**B:** Cardboard. It had a rigid lid, too.

**A:** Then why 'bag'? Tell me again what you carried. Was the box inside something?

First / second pass: 65 / 90

- cardFidelity: original 4 — A1 compares conflicting labels and A3 asks for observable detail, but A5 does not test the new lid evidence. Result 5 — A1 compares inconsistent container descriptions, A3 tests handle material, and A5 repeats the inquiry while testing whether a box was inside a bag.
- responseLogic: original 3 — A3 responds to B2's uncertainty; A5's generic 'build the object' barely uses B4's rigid lid. Result 5 — B2's handles motivate A3's fabric-or-cardboard question; B4's cardboard and rigid lid motivate A5's challenge to 'bag' and nesting hypothesis.
- relationship: original 3 — B2's uncertainty and B4's extra detail support inquiry, but B has no stated practical concern. Result 4 — B2 is uncertain but supplies a remembered action; B4 offers further physical detail that changes A's next question during a shared form task.
- heightening: original 2 — A5 announces a method instead of advancing a concrete consistency test prompted by B4. Result 4 — A5 moves beyond choosing a label to reconstructing what B carried and testing a way the two descriptions might coexist.
- specificity: original 4 — A1's bag-or-box discrepancy and A3's handles are tangible, immediately playable details. Result 5 — A1 establishes a missing-parcel form, and A3 and B4 provide concrete material and lid details actors can interrogate.
- brevityWit: original 3 — A3's noun line is pointed, but A5 ends in an abstract investigative maxim. Result 3 — A3's material question is precise; A5's three questions are useful but more procedural than comically economical.

B4 causes A5: B4 supplies cardboard and a rigid lid; A5 rechecks the bag label and asks about an outer container.

Alternatives: Single-a tests inconsistent descriptions of an object; single-b reconciles apparently conflicting locations before testing the bag's movement.

Reservation: The exchange is deliberately procedural, and A5 contains several questions in one turn. Its evidential progression remains strong. No rewrite needed.

## D126-single-b — revised

Find the truth by comparing details, repeating questions, and testing which parts of the story remain consistent.

Mechanism: A repeats and cross-checks concrete details to establish a truthful sequence, accepting reconciliation when new evidence supports it.

Confusable concept: Catching someone in a lie presumes deception; D126 permits A to find that apparently conflicting details both fit.

### before

**A:** Tell me again what happened between four and four-ten.

**B:** I've already explained.

**A:** Then it should match one of these two versions.

**B:** I stopped for coffee.

**A:** Where does coffee fit into the version with the bus?

### after

**A:** We're claiming for your lost bag. At four, were you on the bus or buying coffee? You've told me both.

**B:** Coffee. Can we finish the form?

**A:** Was the bag beside you when you paid?

**B:** Yes. The driver waited while I ran in.

**A:** So both fit. Tell me the boarding bit again: did the bag come back out with you?

First / second pass: 74 / 90

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 repeats the timeline question; A3 compares versions and A5 tests coffee against the bus account. Result 5 — A1 compares two accounts, A3 tests possession during payment, and A5 revisits boarding after recognizing that both locations can fit.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 turns B2's repetition objection into a consistency demand; A5 incorporates B4's coffee stop. Result 5 — B2 selects coffee, prompting A3's payment question; B4 explains the waiting bus, prompting A5 to reconcile the accounts and test the return journey.
- relationship: original 3 — B2 resists repeating the story, but neither person's stake in the ten minutes is given. Result 4 — B2 wants the claim form finished, while A3 insists on another detail; B4's useful explanation changes A's understanding rather than feeding suspicion.
- heightening: original 3 — A5 makes the comparison more specific, though the supposed bus contradiction remains unstated. Result 4 — A5 accepts B4's reconciliation and narrows the search to whether the bag came back out, advancing the same investigation.
- specificity: original 2 — A1 gives exact times without an incident; A3 references two versions the audience has not heard. Result 5 — A1 establishes a lost-bag claim and four o'clock discrepancy; B4's waiting driver supplies a clear physical sequence.
- brevityWit: original 3 — A3's 'one of these two versions' is pointed but relies on absent information. Result 3 — A5's 'So both fit' cleanly shows revision of belief, though the final question earns clarity more than a strong comic turn.

B4 causes A5: B4 explains the bus waited during the coffee stop; A5 accepts both accounts and investigates reboarding.

Alternatives: This alternative allows cooperation and reconciliation to advance the search; single-a retains uncertainty over the container's identity.

Reservation: A5's 'again' implies an earlier boarding account outside the excerpt. That is plausible conversational context, but the comic intensity is modest. No rewrite needed.
