## D99-single-b — revised

Make someone acknowledge why their action hurt, not merely agree that it happened.

Mechanism: A redirects a logistical explanation toward acknowledgment of the relational fear caused by silence.

Confusable concept: Tell Me I Did Enough seeks reassurance about A's adequacy; this card seeks recognition of why B's action hurt.

### before

**A:** Your silence hurt. Not because I needed an instant reply.

**B:** My phone died.

**A:** I thought our conversation had.

**B:** You thought I'd stopped caring?

**A:** Exactly. Please answer that before the battery story.

### after

**A:** I texted that I'd lost my job. You replied, 'What's for dinner?'

**B:** I thought something normal would help.

**A:** Making your dinner? Can you see why I felt like the cook?

**B:** I meant we could go out. I'll book somewhere.

**A:** I believe you. Before you book, can you see why I heard a chore? I needed you to ask how I was.
