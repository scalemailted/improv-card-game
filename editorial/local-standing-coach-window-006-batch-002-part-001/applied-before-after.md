## D51-single-a — retained

Make someone apologize for the disrespect to your position, not merely for what happened.

Mechanism: A makes B repair disrespect to an office through an apology that explicitly names that office.

Confusable concept: D57 also demands recognition through a title, but does not require an apology for disrespect.

### before

**A:** Your apology should mention interrupting the chair, not just spilling the tea.

**B:** I'm sorry about your trousers.

**A:** The trousers hold no office.

**B:** I'm sorry I interrupted you.

**A:** 'Chair.' Let's repair the title while we're here.

### after

**A:** Your apology should mention interrupting the chair, not just spilling the tea.

**B:** I'm sorry about your trousers.

**A:** The trousers hold no office.

**B:** I'm sorry I interrupted you.

**A:** 'Chair.' Let's repair the title while we're here.

First / second pass: 92 / 92

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 demands an apology for interrupting the chair; A3 rejects compensation directed at trousers. Result 5 — A1 demands an apology for interrupting the chair; A5 rejects personal acknowledgment without the office's title.
- responseLogic: original 5 — B2's trousers produce A3's office distinction; B4's 'you' produces A5's correction to 'Chair.' Result 5 — B2 apologizes for trousers, prompting A3's distinction between clothing and office; B4's 'you' prompts A5's 'Chair.'
- relationship: original 4 — B2 addresses the visible accident and B4 then concedes the interruption, allowing A to narrow the demand. Result 4 — B2 prioritizes the physical mishap, then B4 offers a narrower apology; both require A to clarify the desired respect.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 requires the corrected apology to acknowledge rank after B4 already apologizes personally. Result 4 — After B4 concedes the interruption, A5 makes even the apology's form of address subject to correction.
- specificity: original 5 — A1 supplies a chaired gathering, interruption and spilled tea; B2 establishes whose trousers were hit. Result 4 — A1 supplies a chairperson, interruption and spilled tea; B2 locates the spill on A's trousers.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's 'trousers hold no office' expresses the exact distinction economically; A5 adds one further correction. Result 5 — A3's 'The trousers hold no office' compresses the exact grievance into a playable, character-driven distinction.

B4 causes A5: B4 apologizes for interrupting 'you'; A5 substitutes 'Chair' to make the apology acknowledge office.

Alternatives: Unlike D51-single-b's public repair by email, this alternative makes B's successive spoken apologies undergo precise correction.

Reservation: The institutional setting remains lightly sketched, but the positional grievance is immediately playable. No rewrite needed.
