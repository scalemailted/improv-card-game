## Suggested post-scene questions

- What observable choice did this card give its holder?
- Could the card be played without assuming anything private about the partner?
- What behavior or objective repeated or heightened?
- Which existing card felt closest, and how was this one different?
- Did the wording create confusion, a single joke, or a durable scene engine?
- Would the performer keep, revise, or veto it on a future draw?

## Promotion rule

No pack should move from `live-validation` to `published` solely because its automated audit passes. Promotion requires independent readers, repeated live use, outlier review, and final copy sign-off.
