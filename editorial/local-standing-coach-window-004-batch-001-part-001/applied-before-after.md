## S181-single-a — retained

Mechanism: A treats B's easy insider access as a standard to admire and surpass through unaided mastery.

Confusable concept: Generic perfectionism would explain preparation but would not require B's effortless access or A's rejection of help.

**B:** They know me.

**A:** Right. I'll learn every doorman's birthday. Systematically.

**B:** I can introduce you.

**A:** No, thank you. I'd like to develop spontaneous rapport properly.

### after

**A:** How did you get into the members' lounge without showing your pass?

**B:** They know me.

**A:** Right. I'll learn every doorman's birthday. Systematically.

**B:** I can introduce you.

**A:** No, thank you. I'd like to develop spontaneous rapport properly.

First / second pass: 92 / 88

- responseLogic: original 5 — B2's personal recognition causes A3's birthday research; B4's introduction causes A5's refusal. Result 5 — B2's 'They know me' prompts A3 to study doormen; B4's introduction offer prompts A5 to insist on independently developed rapport.

- heightening: original 4 — A5 commits to independently mastering even the spontaneous rapport offered by B4. Result 4 — A5 turns B4's easy introduction into a demand to master spontaneous rapport, extending A3's systematic social preparation.
- specificity: original 5 — A1 establishes lounge admission without a pass; A3 supplies playable birthday research. Result 5 — A1 establishes a members' lounge and missing pass check; A3 supplies playable doorman-birthday research.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's 'Systematically' prepares A5's sincere contradiction, 'spontaneous rapport properly.' Result 5 — A3's 'Systematically' and A5's 'spontaneous rapport properly' expose the contradiction economically.

Reservation: Admiration and threat are inferred rather than equally explicit. No rewrite needed for this internal pass.
