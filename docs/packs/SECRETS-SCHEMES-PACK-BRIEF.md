# Imprompt Pack Brief — Secrets & Schemes

**Pack ID:** `secrets-schemes`  
**Reserved IDs:** S121–S144 and D121–D144  
**Target release:** Imprompt v0.13.0  
**In-app status:** Playtest  
**Candidate pool:** 64 cards drafted; 48 selected; 8 held; 8 rejected

## Creative promise

**Secrets & Schemes turns concealed information, uncertain motives, investigation, recruitment, and plans under pressure into observable choices.** The pack is not a collection of fixed mysteries or spy scenarios. Each card gives its holder a portable way to manage knowledge: ration it, seek it, trade it, protect it, test it, reinterpret it, or use it to shape the room.

A card belongs when secrecy creates playable interaction. The holder may behave like the only person cleared to know, treat an old cover-up as relationship currency, investigate every inconsistency, recruit someone into a private plan, preserve a kinder version of the truth, or make each failed step look increasingly intentional.

## Central comic question

> **What do I do when information itself becomes the thing everyone needs, fears, protects, or uses?**

The actual secret remains open. It may concern a missing snack, an embarrassing plan, an impossible ritual, a royal succession, a broken prop, or the fate of the universe. The card supplies behavior, not plot.

## What makes this pack distinct

Core Foundations provides broad secrets and objectives. Everyday Friction centers practical strain. Power Games asks who gets to decide. Relationship Knots asks what remains between people. Emotional Pressure asks what feeling reorganizes behavior. Secrets & Schemes focuses on **how knowledge moves through a scene**:

- possession of information may create authority or dependence;
- suspicion may produce investigation, rivalry, crisis, or delight;
- concealment may protect care, reputation, belonging, or control;
- recruitment may turn trust into membership and membership into obligation;
- confession may be sought, delayed, traded, or used as a test;
- a plan may become more elaborate each time reality challenges it;
- evidence may be surrendered, delegated, reframed, or made disproportionately important;
- an old secret may function as shared history, debt, intimacy, or unfinished business.

## Included territory

- secrets, privileged knowledge, incomplete explanations, and selective disclosure;
- investigation, clues, inconsistencies, motives, evidence, and competing theories;
- cover stories, alibis, misdirection, decoys, and reputation management;
- recruitment into plans, inner circles, alliances, and conspiracies;
- confession, admission, accountability, and the timing of truth;
- information as leverage, access, status, care, debt, or currency;
- plans that survive through improvisation, reframing, and escalating complexity;
- suspicion as fear, delight, rivalry, expertise, or grand worldview;
- practical logistics around keeping stories, roles, and details consistent;
- hidden-information behavior that remains legible without explaining the card.

## Excluded territory

This pack should not rely on:

- a mandatory crime, government agency, espionage mission, or detective genre;
- technical instructions for hacking, surveillance, evasion, or destroying evidence;
- declaring that another performer is guilty, lying, blackmailing, or secretly informed;
- psychological abuse presented as a light objective;
- one-note paranoia or diagnostic labels as character shorthand;
- a predetermined culprit, reveal, confession, or ending;
- twists that only work if every performer knows the same hidden fact;
- secret material whose primary engine is emotional trauma rather than behavior;
- institutional procedures better suited to Rules, Rituals & Institutions;
- complex structural reversals better suited to Advanced Scene Engines.

## Portability rule

The card describes the holder’s relationship to information, not the genre or literal contents of the secret.

Too narrow:

> You are a spy trying to recover stolen launch codes.

Portable:

> **Information Is Leverage** — Remain outwardly cooperative while controlling one useful fact, releasing only enough of it to shape each decision.

The portable version works at a family dinner, rehearsal, spaceship, royal court, neighborhood meeting, magical academy, or ordinary trip to the store.

## Hidden-information rule

A Stance may define what the holder suspects, withholds, notices, or values. It cannot declare another performer’s actual knowledge, guilt, motive, or plan.

Weak:

> They know your secret and are blackmailing you.

Strong:

> **Everyone Knows Something** — Interpret private conversations, careful wording, and unexplained pauses as signs that important information is being kept from you.

A Drive may seek information, confession, assistance, endorsement, loyalty, or surrender. It must remain playable when the partner refuses, misunderstands, tells an unexpected truth, or invents a different premise.

## Desired player experience

A player should quickly understand:

1. what information or uncertainty draws the character’s attention;
2. the first visible behavior the prompt creates;
3. what the holder seeks, protects, trades, or redirects;
4. how the behavior can repeat without becoming pure exposition;
5. how offers from the partner can change the theory or plan;
6. how the secret remains discoverable through play rather than announced.

The strongest scenes should feel like two private information strategies colliding, not like two performers waiting to deliver twists.

## Metadata additions

This pack adds six reusable coach roles:

`conspirator`, `informant`, `skeptic`, `witness`, `decoy`, and `strategist`.

It also adds controlled motifs:

`alibi`, `clues`, `confession`, `conspiracy`, `deception`, `evidence`, `information`, `investigation`, `misdirection`, `motive`, `mystery`, `recruitment`, `strategy`, `suspicion`, and `witness`.

These remain internal metadata. The visible seven-category system does not change.

## Exercise value

Secrets & Schemes materially deepens existing coach presets:

- **Status Clash** gains information gatekeepers, initiated insiders, bluffing authorities, and hidden leverage;
- **Crown & Heart** gains tension between control, privileged trust, care, concealment, and the need to be included;
- **Past & Future** gains shared secrets, old cover-ups, unfinished truth, and competing interpretations of evidence;
- **Relationship Pressure** gains protective lies, confidence debts, privileged disclosure, and confession objectives;
- **Pursuer & Avoider** gains its most direct content expansion: investigation, extraction, deflection, and concealment;
- custom exercises can use Conspirator, Informant, Skeptic, Witness, Decoy, and Strategist roles.

## Live-playtest questions

1. Can players reveal the information strategy through behavior instead of explaining the entire secret?
2. Do investigation cards remain responsive when the partner invents an unexpected explanation?
3. Are concealment cards legible enough to create interaction without becoming impossible to read?
4. Does the pack generate ordinary, grounded scenes as readily as mystery or spy genres?
5. Do selected cards remain distinct from existing Core secrets and Power Games leverage prompts?
6. Can beginners heighten a scheme without adding unnecessary plot complexity?
7. Does Pursuer & Avoider gain noticeably more range from the new cards?
8. Are protective deception cards playable without making the holder automatically correct?
9. Which cards are vetoed because they feel ethically uncomfortable versus merely challenging?
10. Do Mirror exercises create satisfying competing theories rather than disconnected monologues?
