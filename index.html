HOW TO PLAY

Four secrets become one scene.

Every performer privately receives a Stance and a Drive. Guided exercises coordinate only the category rules.

  1. 1
    Choose Open Play or an exercise.

    Open Play keeps the full deck available. Mirror gives everyone the same themed pools. Paired gives Player A and Player B complementary assignments.

  2. 2
    Use your own phone.

    Every device keeps an independent shuffle, current scene, and Scene Log. QR codes never synchronize the decks.

  3. 3
    Draw and reveal privately.

    Tap each generic panel to draw and reveal. Tap a revealed card again to Keep or Veto it.

  4. 4
    Play only your information.

    Your Stance directs how you behave. It never dictates another performer’s reaction.

  5. 5
    Let the Drive grow.

    Use the Stance near the top. Allow the Drive to emerge, repeat, and heighten as a slow burn.

  6. 6
    Complete and review.

    Save the final cards to your local Scene Log, then compare phones during the postmortem.

Mirror asks: what happens when both players bring the same kind of pressure?

The cards are still independently drawn, so the same category can create very different private approaches.

Paired asks: what happens when different dramatic functions meet?

Each player sees their own assignment. The coach can make both roles public or preserve the mystery.
