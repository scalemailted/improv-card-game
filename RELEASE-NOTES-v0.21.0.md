# Imprompt v0.21.0 — Concrete Fusion Hints

v0.21.0 changes the two-card coaching panel from parallel Stance and Drive summaries into one integrated, playable demonstration.

A full combination hint now provides:

- **One way to play the pair** — the causal relationship between the selected cards.
- **Your first move** — one observable action that expresses both cards.
- **The repeatable loop** — how the behavior can recur after the scene responds.
- **When the scene changes** — how to bend or release the proposed tactic when a stronger shared pattern emerges.

`Another angle` cycles through Channel, Mask, Friction, Escalation, Reinterpretation, and Counterweight as materially different tactics. The engine remains deterministic, offline, holder-specific, and free of LLM or server inference.

No playable card IDs, titles, instructions, categories, packs, or Scene Log snapshots changed.
