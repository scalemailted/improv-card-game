## S141-single-a — revised

Treat every missing fact, contradiction, and suspicious detail as delightful proof that the situation is becoming more interesting.

Mechanism: A treats absent evidence and conflicting accounts as delightful reasons to become more involved.

Confusable concept: Generic optimism welcomes good outcomes; this card specifically welcomes uncertainty and suspicion.

### before

**A:** A page is missing from the club minutes. What a thoughtful mystery.

**B:** Someone took it.

**A:** And left us a perfectly shaped question.

**B:** We need the budget figures.

**A:** Even better. A mystery with financial consequences.

### after

**A:** The budget page is missing from our club minutes. Someone's made accounts night interesting.

**B:** The treasurer took it home.

**A:** Lovely. A suspect with homework. Shall we ring her?

**B:** She's here. She says she never took it.

**A:** A contradiction in person! Bring her a chair beside mine; I'm staying for the whole meeting.

First / second pass: 80 / 92

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 calls the missing page thoughtful; A5 welcomes financial consequences as added mystery. Result 5 — A1 welcomes the missing page; A3 enjoys a suspect; A5 explicitly delights in the contradiction.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 welcomes B2's theft explanation; A5 explicitly uses B4's budget need. Result 5 — B2 identifies the treasurer, prompting A3's call; B4 places her present and denying it, prompting A5's chair.
- relationship: original 3 — B4 needs budget figures, but A5's appreciation gives B no practical next choice. Result 4 — B2 supplies an ordinary explanation and B4 corrects it; B's personal stake in the accounts remains slight.
- heightening: original 3 — A5 labels B4's consequences interesting without increasing A's involvement. Result 4 — A5 turns B4's live contradiction into a reason to sit beside the treasurer and stay throughout.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 establishes missing club minutes; B4 identifies the needed budget figures. Result 5 — A1 establishes club minutes and accounts night; A5 supplies an immediately playable seating choice.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's 'perfectly shaped question' is concise, though A5 repeats the appreciative tactic. Result 4 — A3's 'suspect with homework' grows directly from B2 taking the page home.

B4 causes A5: B4 makes the treasurer available and introduces her denial; A5 wants her nearby to enjoy the contradiction.

Alternatives: Unlike the anniversary alternative, this uses institutional curiosity and voluntary attendance rather than personal implication.

Reservation: B has limited personal stakes, but no rewrite is needed for an internal pass.

## S142-single-b — revised

Accept that a secret plan exists, then focus on schedules, witnesses, explanations, cleanup, and what everyone must remember.

Mechanism: A supports the secret plan by checking its explanations against schedules, witnesses, and shared recollections.

Confusable concept: An interrogator exposes a false account; A instead helps construct an account that protects an accepted secret.

### before

**A:** Your cover story says you caught the nine o'clock bus.

**B:** Exactly.

**A:** The last bus leaves at eight.

**B:** Then I walked.

**A:** Six miles carrying a cake? We need believable shoes.

### after

**A:** For Mum's surprise party, we're saying you brought the cake on the nine o'clock bus?

**B:** Yes. She'll ask why she didn't see my car.

**A:** The last bus leaves at eight. Can you claim you walked?

**B:** She drove past me at half eight. I was empty-handed.

**A:** Then the cake was already here. We need Dad to remember accepting it before his nap.

First / second pass: 79 / 86

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 accepts a cover story; A3 checks the timetable and A5 tests the replacement explanation. Result 5 — A1 checks the cover story, A3 tests its timetable, and A5 recruits Dad's remembered acceptance.
- responseLogic: original 3 — B2's 'Exactly' supplies no material beyond A1; B4's walking does specifically cause A5. Result 4 — B2 confirms the bus story, which A3 checks; B4's empty-handed sighting forces A5 to separate cake delivery.
- relationship: original 3 — B4 adapts the account, but B2 does little to establish the collaborator's pressure. Result 4 — B2 anticipates Mum's scrutiny and B4 supplies her eyewitness evidence, constraining A's family cover.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 adds physical evidence, believable shoes, to B4's six-mile walking explanation. Result 4 — A5 expands the alibi from B's journey to Dad remembering an earlier delivery.
- specificity: original 4 — A3's last bus at eight and A5's cake make the cover's weaknesses concrete. Result 4 — A1 identifies Mum's surprise party; A3 and B4 provide exact times, while A5 adds Dad's nap.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A5's 'believable shoes' compactly makes clothing part of the cover story. Result 4 — A5's need for Dad to 'remember accepting it' makes mundane family coordination the comic consequence.

B4 causes A5: Mum seeing B empty-handed in B4 defeats carrying the cake; A5 relocates its arrival to an earlier delivery.

Alternatives: The named family witness and remembered delivery distinguish this from the picnic's physical concealment problems.

Reservation: An eight o'clock departure does not strictly exclude a nine o'clock arrival; the local timetable shorthand is imprecise. Internal pass without rewriting.

- specificity: original 4 — A1's doorway to yesterday and B2's old wallpaper establish a visible impossible setting. Result 5 — A1 gives a doorway to yesterday; A5 grounds the impossible dispute in today's mortgage payment.
- brevityWit: original 3 — A3's 'historically receptive entrance' supplies terminology without a consequential correction. Result 4 — A5's visiting hours follow the visitor classification in A3 and the ownership challenge in B4.
