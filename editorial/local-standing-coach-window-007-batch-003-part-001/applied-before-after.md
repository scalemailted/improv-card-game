## D111-single-a — revised

Hide discouraging information because you believe hope is the only thing keeping someone able to continue.

Mechanism: D111 makes A conceal discouraging facts because A believes preserving B's hope is necessary for B to continue.

Confusable concept: Ordinary encouragement can acknowledge all the bad news; this drive requires protective withholding.

### before

**A:** There's still one gallery we haven't tried. Let's pack the paintings.

**B:** What's in the rejection pile?

**A:** Nothing that helps us pack.

**B:** Did the biggest gallery say no?

**A:** The small one hasn't answered. Let's leave it something to say yes to.

### after

**A:** Let's pack your paintings for the last gallery. You've finally picked up a brush again.

**B:** If the big gallery rejected me too, I'm done. Did they write?

**A:** Their letter can wait. This gallery still needs your best three.

**B:** Pick them, then. I can't face another envelope.

**A:** I'll handle the envelopes. You finish the blue one; there's still somewhere for it to go.

First / second pass: 68 / 86

- cardFidelity: original 3 — A3 hides rejection information, but A1–A5 never establish that B needs hope to continue. Result 4 — B2 threatens to quit; A3 postpones the letter and A5 takes control of envelopes, although the rejection remains implied.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 evades B2's rejection pile; A5 redirects B4's biggest-gallery question toward the unanswered application. Result 5 — A3 redirects B2 toward three paintings; A5 answers B4's envelope aversion with a division of work.
- relationship: original 3 — B2 and B4 seek facts, but B's personal investment in continuing remains unspecified. Result 4 — B4 entrusts selection to A, making A a practical supporter with increasing control over information.
- heightening: original 3 — A5 repeats A1's packing proposal with a hopeful justification rather than increasing the concealment's consequence. Result 4 — A3 delays one letter; after B4, A5 assumes responsibility for all envelopes and assigns continued painting.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 supplies paintings and a remaining gallery; B2 makes the rejection pile playable. Result 5 — A1 establishes gallery submissions and resumed painting; A5 supplies the unfinished blue painting.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's 'Nothing that helps us pack' economically converts an information request into practical evasion. Result 4 — A5's 'somewhere for it to go' sustains hope economically, with restrained rather than conspicuous wit.

B4 causes A5: B4 cannot face envelopes and delegates selection; A5 takes over correspondence and gives B a painting task.

Alternatives: Unlike D111-single-b's sacrifice of living space, this alternative protects hope through administrative shielding.

Reservation: The letter's discouraging contents are inferred rather than established. No rewrite needed for an internal pass; reviewed the supplied original.
