## D21-single-a — revised

Remain helpful and encouraging while becoming increasingly furious.

Mechanism: A must keep providing sincere help and encouragement as the same interaction makes A increasingly furious.

Confusable concept: Sarcastic politeness can sound supportive while actually withdrawing encouragement and care.

### before

**A:** You've spilled paint on my sofa. Lovely. I'll fetch a cloth.

**B:** It's on the carpet too.

**A:** Wonderful coverage. You must be thrilled.

**B:** Could you get the blue paint out?

**A:** Absolutely. I'll start with the part that used to be my afternoon.

### after

**A:** You've spilled paint on my sofa. Stay there; I'll get a cloth. We can fix this.

**B:** It's on the carpet too.

**A:** Keep blotting. You're doing well. That carpet was my birthday present.

**B:** The cloth's spreading it.

**A:** You're right. Good catch. Take my clean shirt. I am absolutely furious, but you are not tackling this alone.

First / second pass: 55 / 86

- cardFidelity: original 2 — A1 offers a cloth, but A3's 'You must be thrilled' replaces encouragement with mockery. Result 4 — A3 pairs encouragement with the birthday carpet's loss; A5 supplies a shirt while declaring fury. The anger is more stated than enacted.
- responseLogic: original 3 — A3 uses B2's spreading paint; A5 barely uses B4's request to remove blue paint. Result 5 — B2 extends the spill to the carpet, which A3 addresses; B4 discredits the cloth, so A5 substitutes a clean shirt.
- relationship: original 3 — B4 wants practical cleanup, while A3 mainly ridicules B rather than helping. Result 4 — B4 reports that the attempted repair is failing; A5 credits B's observation and maintains practical solidarity despite personal loss.
- heightening: original 2 — A5 complains about the afternoon without developing the helpfulness or visibly increasing fury. Result 4 — After B4, A5 sacrifices another possession to help, increasing A's commitment while the explicitly expressed anger rises.
- specificity: original 5 — A1's painted sofa and B2's carpet establish an immediately playable domestic accident. Result 5 — A1 immediately establishes spilled paint on A's sofa; A3's birthday carpet and A5's clean shirt provide playable personal stakes.
- brevityWit: original 3 — A3's 'Wonderful coverage' is compact; A5's afternoon metaphor displaces the cleanup. Result 4 — A3's encouragement beside the birthday-present detail earns the tension; A5's explicit emotional declaration explains some of the joke.

B4 causes A5: B4 says the cloth spreads paint; A5 acknowledges the failure and offers a clean shirt instead.

Alternatives: Unlike the birthday chauffeur in D21-single-b, this A faces damage to personal possessions and offers increasingly costly physical help.

Reservation: Accepted as supplied; no rewrite needed. The growing fury relies partly on A5 naming it, rather than on consistently escalating verbal strain.
