## D06-single-a — revised

Probe for what others know while revealing as little as possible.

Mechanism: A tests the extent and reliability of B's knowledge while withholding A's own account.

Confusable concept: Accidentally confessing through suspicious questions can replace the card's effort to minimize disclosure.

### before

**A:** When you say you heard about the party, which part?

**B:** The bit with the cake.

**A:** Before or after it reached the balcony?

**B:** What happened on the balcony?

**A:** Nothing confirmed. Who's been using that word?

### after

**A:** When you say you heard about the party, which part?

**B:** The cake went over the balcony. Was that before you left?

**A:** Who gave you the timing?

**B:** Nobody. Your brother says there's a video.

**A:** Have you watched it, or are we both relying on my brother?

First / second pass: 56 / 92

- cardFidelity: original 2 — A3 volunteers the balcony detail despite the instruction to reveal as little as possible. Result 5 — A3 avoids B2's departure question by probing its source; A5 tests whether B has actually seen the video without confirming events.
- responseLogic: original 3 — A3 uses B2's cake, but A5 asks who used a word that A introduced. Result 5 — A3 picks up B2's timing question; A5 distinguishes viewing evidence from hearsay after B4 introduces the brother's video claim.
- relationship: original 3 — B4 reasonably investigates A3's disclosure, though B has no further stated stake. Result 4 — B2 seeks A's whereabouts and B4 supplies a potential source of proof, making B an active investigator.
- heightening: original 3 — A5 retreats after B4's question, increasing suspicion through a largely self-created problem. Result 4 — A5 responds to the stronger threat of video by narrowing the inquiry to firsthand knowledge while preserving uncertainty.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 establishes party gossip and B2 supplies a cake incident. Result 5 — A1 establishes discussion of a party, and B2's cake over the balcony supplies a vivid incident without A leaking it.
- brevityWit: original 3 — A5's 'Nothing confirmed' is compact, but its source question depends on an obvious self-own. Result 4 — A5's 'are we both relying on my brother' deftly suggests shared uncertainty while avoiding an account of A's own actions.

B4 causes A5: B4 attributes a video's existence to A's brother; A5 asks whether B watched it or only heard about it.

Alternatives: Alternative a distinguishes hearsay from video evidence in a family-linked party incident; b narrows eyewitness knowledge under workplace suspicion.

Reservation: The humor is understated and A3's source question is conspicuously evasive, but both serve the intended behavior. Retain unchanged.

## D06-single-b — revised

Probe for what others know while revealing as little as possible.

Mechanism: A answers scrutiny with narrower questions that map the evidence while withholding an explanation.

Confusable concept: A generic evasive denial would conceal information without actively finding out what B knows.

### before

**A:** What did the manager say about the missing keys?

**B:** That they're missing.

**A:** No mention of a drain, then?

**B:** Should there be?

**A:** I'm checking how thorough the briefing was.

### after

**A:** What did the manager say about the missing keys?

**B:** They might be in the drain. You locked up, didn't you?

**A:** Is she asking everyone who locked up, or just me?

**B:** Just you. Someone saw you outside.

**A:** Did they say what I was doing, or only where I was?

First / second pass: 57 / 95

- cardFidelity: original 2 — A3 needlessly introduces a drain; A5 stops probing and merely explains the question away. Result 5 — A3 probes whether scrutiny is targeted without confirming lockup; A5 tests the witness's knowledge without supplying A's actions.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 tests B2's minimal report and A5 deflects B4's suspicion coherently. Result 5 — A3 follows B2's lockup question; A5 splits location from conduct after B4 says someone saw A outside.
- relationship: original 3 — B4 challenges the suspicious detail, but B2 offers little independent stake. Result 4 — B2 directly questions A and B4 reports targeted suspicion, giving A a credible reason to limit disclosures.
- heightening: original 2 — A5 retreats to a generic briefing explanation without a sharper information request. Result 5 — B4 moves from possible location of the keys to an eyewitness placing A outside; A5 responds with a finer test of incriminating detail.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 establishes a manager and missing keys; A3 specifies a possible location. Result 5 — A1's manager and missing keys establish work stakes, while B2's drain and B4's witness make the inquiry concrete.
- brevityWit: original 3 — A5 is economical but relies on the familiar accidental-disclosure joke created in A3. Result 4 — A5's distinction between 'what I was doing' and 'where I was' crisply exposes defensive precision without a separate joke.

B4 causes A5: B4 reveals that A is singled out because of a sighting; A5 checks whether the witness reported conduct as well as location.

Alternatives: Alternative b creates targeted workplace jeopardy; a works through a brother's hearsay and the possibility of a video.

Reservation: A3 assumes B's question reflects the manager's inquiry; B4 supports that reading. The wit depends on delivery of A's precise evasions. Retain unchanged.
