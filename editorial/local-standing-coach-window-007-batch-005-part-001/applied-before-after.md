## D131-single-b — revised

Use increasingly sensitive details to test whether someone deserves deeper trust.

Mechanism: A uses successively more consequential disclosures to test whether B merits a more intimate confidence.

Confusable concept: Simply delaying an explanation can resemble this card without actually testing trust through increasing sensitivity.

### before

**A:** Here's a small secret: I withdrew my application.

**B:** Why?

**A:** First, where does that sentence go after you leave?

**B:** Nowhere.

**A:** Then it may be safe to give it a reason.

### after

**A:** I withdrew my college application. Have you told Mum?

**B:** She asked. I said my sister's plans were hers to explain.

**A:** Then there's more. I've leased a bakery. Can I send you the address?

**B:** Call me. Mum uses my tablet; she'd see the message.

**A:** Good catch. I'll call. It's Mum's old bakery. I want you there when I tell her.

First / second pass: 55 / 92

- cardFidelity: original 2 — A1 supplies one secret; A5 merely promises a reason rather than sharing something more sensitive. Result 5 — A1 checks whether B told Mum, A3 discloses the bakery lease after B's discretion, and A5 reveals its family significance after another safeguard.
- responseLogic: original 4 — B2's why prompts A3's confidentiality question, and B4's assurance licenses A5's conditional offer. Result 5 — B2's defense of A's privacy earns A3's 'Then there's more'; B4's shared-tablet warning directly produces A5's agreement to call.
- relationship: original 3 — B2 shows curiosity, but B4's unqualified nowhere gives A no meaningful relational complication. Result 4 — B2 establishes sibling loyalty while B4 contributes a credible household privacy problem; B supports A without becoming passive.
- heightening: original 2 — A5 stops before the deeper disclosure that would develop A1's trust test. Result 4 — A5 reveals that the leased property is Mum's old bakery and asks B to attend the disclosure, increasing emotional involvement.
- specificity: original 3 — A1 names an application but leaves its purpose and B's connection unspecified. Result 5 — A1 supplies the college decision and family stakes; B4's shared tablet gives the secrecy an immediately playable practical obstacle.
- brevityWit: original 3 — A3's sentence going somewhere is concise, but A5 remains abstract and anticipatory. Result 4 — A3's address offer economically sets up B4's tablet warning and A5's family revelation; the effect is warm recognition more than a sharp joke.

B4 causes A5: B4 protects the secret from the shared tablet; A5 accepts the safer channel and entrusts B with the bakery's identity and a support role.

Alternatives: D131-single-a tests club confidentiality and document custody; this scene develops sibling solidarity through a concrete privacy safeguard and family history.

Reservation: No rewrite needed. A5 shifts partly from testing trust to granting it; the progression remains clear, though the comic pressure is gentle.
