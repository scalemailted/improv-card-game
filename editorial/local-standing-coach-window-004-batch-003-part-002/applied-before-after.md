## S205-single-a — revised

Treat the ease and confidence success seems to give others as both inspiring and threatening, then sharpen your need to outperform them.

Mechanism: S205 makes another person's apparent ease both a model and a threat, driving A to outperform that very ease.

Confusable concept: Vanity seeks an attractive appearance without necessarily borrowing a successful rival's confidence as its competitive target.

### before

**A:** You looked so casual collecting that prize. I'll collect mine without even standing fully up.

**B:** You haven't won one yet.

**A:** I'm preparing a more effortless response.

**B:** You're practising it in a mirror.

**A:** Only until it looks less prepared than yours.

### after

**A:** You sold every painting without looking busy. Book me the bigger room.

**B:** Start with six. I'll help you hang them.

**A:** Twelve. Show me that relaxed lean you did beside the prices.

**B:** I could relax because you handled all my sales.

**A:** Then I'll sell my own twelve, glass in hand. You just keep me topped up.
