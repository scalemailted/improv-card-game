# Imprompt v0.18.0 — Amber Subtheme Review

## Scope and limitation

The 20 subthemes rated Amber in the v0.17 final-library audit.

This was an **internal metadata-blind editorial review**: card titles and instructions were assessed without using their pack/category labels as an excuse for similarity. It also included a structured opposite-deck pairing gauntlet. It is reproducible editorial evidence, but it does **not** replace independent human blind readers or live scene exposure.

| Measure | Result |
|---|---:|
| Amber subthemes reviewed | 20 of 20 |
| Cards reviewed | 200 |
| Structured pairings assessed | 1200 |
| Pairings with premise conflicts | 0 |
| External human gate retained | Yes |

## Method

For each Amber subtheme:

1. The ten cards sharing the subtheme were read without pack/category framing.
2. Each card was checked for an observable first move, a repeatable or pursuable engine, and holder-only hidden-information language.
3. Each card was assessed against six deliberately varied opposite-deck prompts spanning grounded and heightened/absurd tone, low and high intensity, and both toward-partner and away/against-partner orientations.
4. Pairings were classified as immediately playable, playable with deliberate integration, or premise-conflicting.
5. Residual similarities were documented rather than silently promoted to publication.

## Publication interpretation

Together with 19 Green subthemes carried forward and 9 Red subthemes addressed through targeted rewrites, this review completes a 48-subtheme internal readiness matrix. A passing internal Amber review moves a pack to **live-validation**, not `published`. Each affected subtheme must still receive independent blind reads, repeated live exposures, veto/abandonment review, and final copy sign-off.
