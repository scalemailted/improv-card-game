## S191-single-a — revised

Choose one tiny procedural detail—wording, order, timing, seating, or a mark—and treat it as the only source of legitimacy.

Mechanism: A treats one misplaced mark as the sole source of a marriage's official legitimacy despite twenty years together.

Confusable concept: Ordinary proofreading would correct the certificate without changing when A believes the marriage begins.

### before

**A:** This marriage certificate has the dot above the wrong letter.

**B:** We've been married twenty years.

**A:** Socially, certainly. Typographically, it's complicated.

**B:** We have three children.

**A:** Then let's correct it before they inherit the error.

### after

**A:** This marriage certificate has the dot above the wrong letter.

**B:** We've been married twenty years.

**A:** Twenty years together. But until that dot moves, this doesn't certify a marriage.

**B:** Then move it. Our anniversary party starts at seven.

**A:** Certainly. But we'll need a wedding cake. This dot makes tonight our first day.

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 identifies one misplaced dot; A3 makes the marriage's standing depend on typography. Result 5 — A3 explicitly denies certification until the dot moves; A5 makes that single mark determine the marriage's starting date.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 answers B2's twenty years with 'Socially'; A5 extends B4's children into inheritance. Result 5 — B2's twenty years becomes A3's distinction between togetherness and marriage; B4's correction and anniversary party become A5's wedding celebration.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 and B4 defend their shared marriage through duration and children. Result 4 — B2 asserts their shared history, and B4 offers a practical fix under a party deadline, giving A a cooperative route forward.
- heightening: original 4 — B4 prompts A5 to extend the supposed defect into the next generation. Result 5 — After B4 authorizes moving the dot, A5 converts an anniversary into the first day of marriage, increasing the detail's consequences.
- specificity: original 5 — A1's marriage certificate and B2's twenty years immediately establish spouses and stakes. Result 5 — A1 supplies a marriage certificate and misplaced dot; B4 adds a party at seven, making the immediate situation easy to play.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's 'Socially, certainly' efficiently preserves sincerity; A5's inheritance follows B4. Result 4 — A3's 'Twenty years together' economically establishes the distinction that earns A5's demand for a wedding cake.

B4 causes A5: B4 permits correction and mentions tonight's anniversary party; A5 treats correction as creating a marriage and changes the required cake.

Alternatives: The preserved S191-single-b uses a club administrator's helpful correction and retrospective financial liability; this alternative uses a spouse's correction to redefine shared history.

Reservation: The clerical premise is deliberately implausible, but its consequences remain consistent. No rewrite needed.
