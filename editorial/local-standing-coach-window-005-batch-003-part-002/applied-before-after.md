## D14-single-a — revised

Choose one subject you cannot let the scene reach. Redirect whenever the conversation gets close.

Mechanism: D14 makes A repeatedly steer conversation and immediate plans away from one forbidden subject as B approaches it.

Confusable concept: Helpful Culprit would make A investigate the car damage; this A uses hospitality to prevent the damage becoming the topic.

### before

**A:** Before we discuss the dent in your car, I've found a lovely garage café.

**B:** Did you hit a bollard?

**A:** Their scones are enormous. Shall I book a table?

**B:** Why does the booking need a mechanic?

**A:** Excellent question. Let's inspect their staffing over tea.

### after

**A:** I've booked your car into that garage with the café. Shall we have breakfast there?

**B:** Why does my car need a garage?

**A:** Their full breakfast includes unlimited toast. You always say that's important.

**B:** I'll look at the car while you order.

**A:** Let's take the bus. Then we can both have the breakfast cocktails.

First / second pass: 71 / 88

- cardFidelity: original 4 — A3 dodges B2's bollard question through scones, and A5 diverts B4 into staffing, although A1 openly names the dent. Result 5 — A3 avoids B2's question about the car; A5 counters B4's inspection with another breakfast-focused diversion.
- responseLogic: original 3 — A3 avoids answering B2 coherently, but B4 assumes a booking requires a mechanic when A3 offered only a café table. Result 4 — B2 triggers A3's toast distraction; B4's plan to inspect the car prompts A5's bus-and-cocktails alternative.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 seeks accountability for damage to B's car, and B4 tries to connect A's hospitality to a repair. Result 4 — B2 wants an explanation for work on B's car and B4 takes initiative to discover it directly.
- heightening: original 3 — A5 repeats the tea diversion after B4 without making the forbidden topic appreciably harder to avoid. Result 4 — A5 expands A3's conversational distraction into a proposal controlling travel and shared activity.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 establishes car damage and a garage café; A3's table booking is playable, though B4's mechanic lacks setup. Result 5 — A1 naturally joins a garage booking to its café; A3 exploits B's known enthusiasm for unlimited toast.
- brevityWit: original 3 — A3's enormous scones are a clear diversion; A5's 'inspect their staffing' sounds constructed to service B4's unexplained mechanic. Result 4 — A5's breakfast cocktails extend A3's food lure and provide a motivated reason to abandon driving.

B4 causes A5: B plans to inspect the car alone; A proposes shared bus travel and cocktails to keep B engaged elsewhere.

Alternatives: Alternative a uses personalized enticement and practical arrangements; b jumps between visible distractions.

Reservation: No rewrite needed. Taking the bus does not itself prevent B inspecting the car; A5 remains an attempted diversion.

## D14-single-b — revised

Choose one subject you cannot let the scene reach. Redirect whenever the conversation gets close.

Mechanism: D14 makes A protect a forbidden topic by finding a new conversational exit whenever B brings the current diversion close to it.

Confusable concept: General distractibility would not repeatedly move away from the same financial exposure.

### before

**A:** Don't read that letter yet. Have you noticed the new curtains?

**B:** The letter says the rent's overdue.

**A:** Exactly. Think how much these curtains improve the room.

**B:** How much did they cost?

**A:** Look at that bird outside. Perfectly framed.

### after

**A:** Don't open that rent letter yet. Help me hang our new curtains.

**B:** The landlord wants an answer today.

**A:** He'll love how we're looking after the place. Let's get a photo of the curtains.

**B:** I'll send him one and ask whether we're behind.

**A:** Not with that carpet showing. Let's rearrange the room first. You move the sofa; I'll hold your phone.

First / second pass: 90 / 95

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 steers away from the letter, A3 evades B2's overdue-rent disclosure, and A5 changes subject again when B4 asks about spending. Result 5 — A1 delays the rent letter through curtains; A3 diverts the landlord's deadline into a photograph; A5 diverts the arrears inquiry into rearranging furniture.
- responseLogic: original 5 — A3 returns to the curtains after B2 identifies rent; B4 makes those curtains financially dangerous, causing A5 to move attention through the window. Result 5 — B2's required landlord response inspires A3's photo response; B4 repurposes that photo to ask about arrears, causing A5 to delay photography and claim the phone.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 reads the consequential letter despite A's diversion, and B4 follows the money through A's chosen alternative subject. Result 4 — B2 insists on today's deadline and B4 combines A's decorating idea with the necessary inquiry, actively defeating A's first diversion.
- heightening: original 4 — B4 turns A's safe curtain topic into a spending question, forcing A5 into a more conspicuous second escape. Result 5 — After B4 turns the photo into a route to the forbidden subject, A5 escalates to moving the sofa and taking custody of B's phone.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 supplies a letter and new curtains; B2 locates the financial problem and A5 uses the established window area. Result 5 — A1 naturally establishes shared housing, a rent letter and new curtains; A5 gives actors concrete furniture and a phone to negotiate over.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A5's 'Perfectly framed' carries the curtains into the new bird diversion, making the escape connected rather than wholly random. Result 4 — A5's 'I'll hold your phone' follows precisely from B4's plan to contact the landlord, earning the comic reveal of the practical diversion.

B4 causes A5: B4 will send the photo and ask about arrears; A5 makes taking that photo contingent on rearranging the room and offers to hold the communication device.

Alternatives: The preserved car scene diverts inquiry through shared treats and transport; this scene uses domestic improvement and B's cooperative adaptation to increase the obstruction.

Reservation: A5 makes A's obstruction fairly transparent; it remains playable as sincere insistence on presenting the home properly. No rewrite needed.
