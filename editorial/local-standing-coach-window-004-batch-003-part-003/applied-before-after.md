## S208-single-b — revised

Treat every choice around you as a risk whose consequences may land on you. Press for a voice in decisions you cannot control.

Mechanism: S208 makes each new choice expose another consequence A may inherit, prompting a further claim to decision-making power.

Confusable concept: Territorial possessiveness defends control for its own sake; A here ties requested control to personally inherited repair work.

### before

**A:** You're inviting thirty people to camp in our garden? I maintain the lawn.

**B:** It's only one weekend.

**A:** Grass experiences time differently. I want a say in tent placement.

**B:** You're making this complicated.

**A:** I'm trying to keep your simple weekend out of my next six Saturdays.

### after

**A:** Thirty people camping in our garden? I maintain that lawn.

**B:** It's only one weekend.

**A:** Followed by six Saturdays of reseeding. I want a say in tent placement.

**B:** Fine. Put them wherever you like, but leave room for the fire pit.

**A:** Then I need a say in that too. You've given me the seating plan for my next repair job.
