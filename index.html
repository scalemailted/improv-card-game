HOW TO PLAY

Four secrets become one scene.

Every performer privately receives a Stance and a Drive. No one sees anyone else’s prompts.

  1. 1
    Open your own deck.

    Each improver opens Imprompt on their own phone. Decks are independent and never synchronized.

  2. 2
    Choose how random should work.

    Leave each card on Random All, or focus the Stance or Drive on one category for a coached exercise.

  3. 3
    Draw and reveal privately.

    Tap each generic panel to draw and reveal its prompt. A second tap opens Keep or Veto.

  4. 4
    Play only your information.

    Your Stance directs how you behave. It never dictates how another performer must react.

  5. 5
    Let the Drive grow.

    Use the Stance near the top of the scene. Allow the Drive to emerge, repeat, and heighten as a slow burn.

  6. 6
    Complete and review.

    Mark the scene complete to save its final cards in your private Scene Log for the postmortem.

Random All stays broad. Category draws stay focused.

Used on its own, Random All avoids repeats until the full deck is exhausted. A focused category cycles only after every card in that category has been used.

Contradictions are fuel.

Your character’s belief may be wrong, and two players may privately understand the relationship in completely different ways.
