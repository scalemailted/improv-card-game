## D141-single-a — revised

Remain open, caring, and reassuring while revealing less with every new question.

Mechanism: A meets successive questions with progressively less information while maintaining affectionate availability and reassurance.

Confusable concept: Generic evasion can avoid questions without progressively reducing disclosure or sustaining care.

### before

**A:** I'm so glad you asked about the meeting. Have some tea.

**B:** What happened?

**A:** Nothing you need to worry about.

**B:** Who was there?

**A:** Lovely people. More tea?

### after

**A:** Your school meeting went fine, love. The headteacher and I discussed your suspension. Have some tea.

**B:** How long am I suspended?

**A:** A little while. I'll be home with you.

**B:** Will I miss the school play?

**A:** Oh, sweetheart. Come sit beside me. You don't have to face this alone.

First / second pass: 57 / 92

- cardFidelity: original 3 — A3 offers no meeting facts, so A5's 'Lovely people' does not clearly reveal less. Result 5 — A1 names the suspension; A3 supplies only 'a little while'; A5 replaces information with affectionate reassurance.
- responseLogic: original 3 — A3 evades B2's question; B4 changes the question but barely changes A5's tea tactic. Result 5 — B2 asks duration, prompting A3's vague duration; B4 names the play, prompting A5's comfort without an answer.
- relationship: original 3 — B2 and B4 seek information, but neither establishes why this meeting matters to B. Result 4 — B2 and B4 pursue concrete consequences while A3 offers parental company and A5 physical proximity.
- heightening: original 2 — A5 repeats A1's tea offer without a clear further contraction of disclosure. Result 4 — B4 raises a personally important consequence; A5 increases emotional support while withholding even a yes or no.
- specificity: original 3 — A1 supplies a meeting and tea, but no particular relationship or consequence. Result 5 — A1 establishes a parent returning from a suspension meeting; B4 makes the child's concern specific through the school play.
- brevityWit: original 3 — A3's reassurance and A5's tea are concise but familiar evasions. Result 4 — A3's 'A little while' and A5's disproportionate consolation make the withholding legible without explanation.

B4 causes A5: B4 identifies the school play as a possible loss; A5 treats that concern as requiring comfort while withholding its factual resolution.

Alternatives: Unlike the depot alternative's service relationship, this version uses parental intimacy and a child's personal loss.

Reservation: A5's grave reassurance could imply worse news than the established suspension, though it remains playable within the situation. No rewrite needed.

- cardFidelity: original 4 — A1 prices a name against a truth, but A5 reduces the purchased name to a surname without explaining equality. Result 5 — A1 prices names against names; A3 reprices the exchange as departments; A5 matches an introduction with an introduction.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 uses B2's claim of knowledge as proof of stock; A5 prices B4's offered caller identity. Result 5 — B2's limited knowledge produces A3's cheaper trade; B4's department and source access each receive a counterpart in A5.
- relationship: original 3 — B4 bargains, but B2 never clarifies what B needs the name for. Result 4 — B2 negotiates from limited information and B4 improves the offer, giving B agency within the coworkers' exchange.
- heightening: original 3 — A5 subdivides the product but does not establish why the offer merits only a surname. Result 4 — B4 offers source access, moving A5 beyond trading labels into arranging reciprocal meetings.
- specificity: original 2 — A1's 'useful name' and B4's 'who called' lack a playable shared situation. Result 4 — A1 establishes reciprocal lateness reports; B4 and A5 supply Payroll, Marketing and coffee as playable details.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's 'product exists' makes the information market clear in a short reply. Result 4 — A3's 'Names cost names' expresses the valuation efficiently; A5's final price summary slightly repeats the demonstrated exchange.

B4 causes A5: B4 supplies Payroll and offers an introduction; A5 supplies Marketing and reciprocal source access.

Alternatives: This alternative uses cooperative workplace barter; the sibling alternative prices confessions by punishment and ends with a shared personal connection.

Reservation: The perfectly matched sources make the bargain somewhat tidy, and A5 explains the equality after demonstrating it. No rewrite needed.

- brevityWit: original 3 — A5 explains the precision joke instead of embodying a fresh restriction. Result 5 — A3's 'You didn't ask what was inside' and A5's 'different arrival' earn their turns through progressively literal honesty.
