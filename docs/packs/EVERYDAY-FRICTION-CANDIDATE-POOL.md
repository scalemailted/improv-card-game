This pool records the larger drafting set used to choose S25–S48 and D25–D48. It contains **64 candidates: 48 selected, 8 held, and 8 rejected**. The machine-readable source is `cards/candidates/everyday-friction-candidate-pool.json`.

## Selection principles

The selected set was preferred when it offered a clearer first move, a more repeatable pattern, stronger portability, less overlap with Core Foundations, and cleaner category identity. “Held” means the idea remains usable after revision or may belong in a later official pack. “Rejected” means the current wording or engine should not advance.

This is an editorial drafting record, not evidence of independent blind reads or live troupe performance. Those gates remain part of Pack 2 playtesting.
