## D161-single-a — revised

Use each failed step to reveal a different goal the plan was secretly pursuing, then act immediately on that new mission.

Mechanism: Each failed operation reveals a supposedly concealed objective, which A immediately begins pursuing.

Confusable concept: Optimistic reframing could celebrate failure without replacing the plan's intended goal.

### before

**A:** The map didn't find a door. Our real mission is waking this wall.

**B:** So the map failed?

**A:** At doors. Knock here; the wall looks asleep.

**B:** It's not responding.

**A:** Then we're meant to listen through it. Put your ear down.

### after

**A:** The treasure map ends at this solid wall. It secretly led us here to wake it. I'm knocking.

**B:** Nothing. And our torch is nearly out.

**A:** Then we were sent to hear it in darkness. I'm putting the torch out.

**B:** I can only hear you breathing.

**A:** That's it. The map was testing our breath control. I'm holding mine.

First / second pass: 65 / 89

- cardFidelity: original 4 — A1 replaces finding a door with waking a wall; A5 replaces waking with listening, but B2 prompts no new mission. Result 5 — A1 turns the blocked route into waking the wall; A3 and A5 reveal replacement missions and immediately extinguish the torch and hold breath.
- responseLogic: original 3 — A3 answers B2 with 'At doors' but repeats A1's mission; B4's nonresponse does cause A5's listening. Result 5 — B2 reports no response and a dying torch, prompting A3's darkness mission; B4 hears only breathing, prompting A5's breath-control mission.
- relationship: original 2 — B2 questions failure and B4 reports silence, without establishing why B needs this expedition to succeed. Result 3 — B2 supplies dwindling resources and B4 reports what is audible, but B expresses little personal purpose beyond accompanying A.
- heightening: original 3 — A5 changes the activity to listening, but adds little commitment beyond A3's knocking. Result 4 — A3 sacrifices their light; A5 makes A's own breathing the next instrument of the mission after B4's observation.
- specificity: original 3 — A1 supplies a map and wall, but no destination or practical reason to find the door. Result 5 — A1 establishes treasure hunters stopped at a solid wall; B2's torch gives the actors a concrete shared predicament.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's 'At doors' economically limits the admitted failure; A5 stays within the wall premise. Result 4 — A3's extinguishing the useful torch and A5's holding breath economically express sincere, increasingly counterproductive certainty.

B4 causes A5: B4 identifies breathing as the sole sound; A5 treats that exact result as evidence of a breath-control test.

Alternatives: Unlike the radio operators in alternative b, these explorers lose practical resources through A's own actions, making the escalation more consequential.

Reservation: B remains chiefly an observer; a stronger individual stake could enrich the interaction. No rewrite needed for this internal pass.
