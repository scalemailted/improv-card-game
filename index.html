HOW TO PLAY

Two private realities. One scene.

  1. 1

    Each improver opens the app on their own phone and draws a private Stance and Drive.

  2. 2

    Use the Stance as your opening point of view. Let the Drive emerge as a slow burn.

  3. 3

    Never quote or announce a card. Make it readable through choices, behavior, and reactions.

  4. 4

    After the scene, guess what the other character believed or wanted—then reveal the cards.

A card is subjective.

Your character may be completely wrong. Contradictory assumptions are fuel, not errors.
