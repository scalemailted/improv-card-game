**A:** Which photo should go on the reunion poster? Mine or Sam's?

**B:** Sam's is sharper.

**A:** Sharpness first. Would you at least show the committee mine first?

**A:** Equal treatment. After three weekends helping you move. Could mine at least be the attachment they open first? Just readability.

- heightening: original 4 — After B4 challenges the insinuation, A5 raises the stakes from photographic preference to repayment of personal help. Result 4 — A5 invokes three weekends of moving help and narrows the test to opening order after B4 removes size and sending priority.
