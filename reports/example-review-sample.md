# v0.23.0 exact-pair editorial sample

Internal authoring review, not independent human or live-troupe evaluation. These twelve pairs were checked against the exact instructions. Six extra pairs discovered during the final sample pass had disjoint compiled examples and were replaced with bespoke records. This is evidence that the flag-and-override workflow matters; it is not evidence that the unreviewed corpus is free of similar issues.

All examples below are authored/stored text, not browser-model output.

## S124+D101

**Ask for inclusion through disclosure**

> [I pull up a chair beside the sealed announcement.] “If I belong in this meeting, read me the difficult part first. I should not have to hear it secondhand.”

**Link initiation to who speaks first**

> [I place my badge on the unopened letter.] “You have the clearance to say what is in here. Include me by saying it, not by making me open it alone.”


## S67+D101

## S22+D14

## S192+D74

## S01+D13

## S224+D102

> [I write my name beneath yours on the IOU.] “Every reassuring phrase adds another favour. Tell me the awkward part before I owe you twice.”
