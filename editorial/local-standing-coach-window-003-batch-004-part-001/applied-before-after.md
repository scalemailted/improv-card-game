## S163-single-a — revised

Treat every ordinary step as only the beginning of an elaborate ceremony, adding necessary details whenever anyone tries to finish.

Mechanism: A converts an ordinary step into ceremony and makes each proposed finish depend on another necessary ceremonial detail.

Confusable concept: An elaborate existing ritual may simply have many rules; this card invents additional necessities in response to attempts to finish.

### before

**A:** The final candle needs a witness candle before we end.

**B:** Can that be the last candle?

**A:** Once we acknowledge its service.

**B:** How do we do that?

**A:** Light the acknowledgement candle. We're nearly at the beginning of finishing.

### after

**A:** Before you blow out your birthday candle, we need a witness candle.

**B:** Can that be the last one? The ice cream's melting.

**A:** Yes, once we acknowledge its service.

**B:** I'll say thank you. Then we eat.

**A:** Lovely. Light the acknowledgement candle so everyone can see you thanking it.

First / second pass: 71 / 95

- cardFidelity: original 3 — A3 and A5 add ceremonial requirements at attempted closure, but A1 starts inside an existing candle ceremony rather than an ordinary task. Result 5 — A1 adds a witness candle; A3 conditions finishing on acknowledgement; A5 adds a candle for that acknowledgement.
- responseLogic: original 5 — B2's last-candle request triggers A3's acknowledgment condition; B4's method question directly triggers A5's additional candle. Result 5 — B2 asks for the last candle, generating A3's condition; B4 offers spoken thanks, generating A5's lighting requirement.
- relationship: original 3 — B2 wants an endpoint and B4 cooperates, though the reason for finishing remains unspecified. Result 4 — B2 wants to save melting ice cream and B4 cooperates with the ritual to reach eating.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 makes acknowledgment require another candle, extending the very process B2 hoped to end. Result 5 — A5 makes B4's attempted completion require another instance of the very object B2 wanted to stop adding.
- specificity: original 2 — A1 names a final candle but never establishes what ordinary activity the participants are completing. Result 4 — A1 establishes a birthday candle and B2 supplies melting ice cream, making both the ritual and pressure immediately playable.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A5's 'beginning of finishing' earns its paradox through the added acknowledgment candle. Result 5 — A3's short condition and A5's acknowledgement candle create a precise recursive obstruction without explanation.

B4 causes A5: B4 offers thanks as the acknowledgement; A5 requires a new candle to illuminate those thanks.

Alternatives: Alternative a exploits cooperative thanks and recursive props; b turns urgent departure into a formal relationship between knots.

Reservation: No rewrite needed. A3's 'its service' leaves the precise candle referent slightly loose.
