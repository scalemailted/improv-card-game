## D31-single-a — retained

Recruit someone to support your version of how an ordinary responsibility should be handled.

Mechanism: A recruits a housemate to corroborate A's preferred allocation of an ordinary shared responsibility.

Confusable concept: This could resemble generic lying or avoiding chores unless recruiting B's support remains central.

### before

**A:** At the house meeting, back me up about the bins being Alex's turn.

**B:** I thought it was yours.

**A:** Only on the old rota. Remember the new conversation?

**B:** There was a new conversation?

**A:** We're having it now. Try to remember it confidently.

### after

**A:** At the house meeting, back me up about the bins being Alex's turn.

**B:** I thought it was yours.

**A:** Only on the old rota. Remember the new conversation?

**B:** There was a new conversation?

**A:** We're having it now. Try to remember it confidently.

First / second pass: 90 / 92

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 recruits corroboration about Alex's bin duty; A3 and A5 manufacture support for that allocation. Result 5 — A1 recruits support for assigning the bins to Alex; A3 and A5 manufacture a shared account supporting that assignment.
- responseLogic: original 5 — B2's conflicting recollection prompts A3's new rota; B4's ignorance prompts A5 to establish the conversation now. Result 5 — B2 remembers A's turn, prompting A3's replacement rota; B4 questions the conversation, prompting A5 to designate this exchange as it.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 remembers A being responsible and B4 questions the supposed agreement, complicating recruitment. Result 4 — B2 and B4 give the prospective ally an independent memory, though B4 offers little resistance beyond surprise.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 moves from citing a past agreement in A3 to creating one that B must remember confidently. Result 4 — A5 escalates from citing an alleged agreement in A3 to recruiting B into creating and confidently remembering one.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 gives a house meeting, named housemate and bin duty; A3 supplies the rota dispute. Result 4 — A1 establishes a house meeting, bins and Alex's disputed turn; A3's 'new conversation' is deliberately vague.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A5's 'We're having it now' economically converts B4's question into the attempted corroboration. Result 5 — A5's 'We're having it now' economically turns B4's doubt into the very evidence A needs.

B4 causes A5: B4 doubts the new conversation exists; A5 makes the current conversation the proposed replacement agreement.

Alternatives: This alternative recruits a housemate into constructing shared history; D31-single-b bargains down a colleague's explicit refusal to lie.

Reservation: B's personal stake in the rota remains implicit. No rewrite needed.
