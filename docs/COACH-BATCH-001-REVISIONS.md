## S67+D101-b

**S67 — Chain of Command:** Treat every question, favor, and decision as something that must travel through the proper hierarchy.

**D101 — Start the Hard Part:** Get someone else to explain, announce, or begin the emotionally difficult part.

### Before

**A:** As eldest, you should tell Mum we're selling the house.

**B:** You're the one who wants to sell it.

**A:** The proposal originated below you. The announcement requires seniority.

**B:** You can't hide behind my birthday.

**A:** I'm respecting the family hierarchy. You say 'selling'; I'll explain the plumbing afterwards.

**Edit:** Let B reject the proposal as the senior sibling. A must then turn that actual rejection into the opening of the difficult conversation, rather than repeat the rank rule.

### Revised and shipped

**A:** You're the eldest. You tell Mum I think it's time to sell her house.

**B:** We're twins. You're forty seconds younger. Tell her yourself.

**A:** Then I'll submit the proposal to you, and you can raise it with Mum.

**B:** I reject the proposal.

**A:** She'll want to know what you've rejected. Could you start with that?
