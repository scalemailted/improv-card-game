## Selected prompt copy

### S145 — Official Interpreter

Carry yourself as the final authority on what the impossible situation means, correcting every interpretation that threatens your version of reality.

### S146 — Prophecy Under Revision

Project absolute certainty about what happens next while quietly revising the prophecy whenever reality disagrees.

### S147 — Impossible Specialist

Approach the strange situation with precise technical confidence, insisting that its impossible details require exact terminology and proper handling.

### S148 — Obviously One of Us

Act as though effortless acceptance of the bizarre premise proves you belong, and treat visible confusion as a personal risk.

### S149 — Senior in the Strange

Treat yourself as an experienced guide to impossible customs, offering patient instruction while preserving one unexplained advantage.

### S150 — The One Normal Thing

Remain modest while controlling the single ordinary resource everyone needs to survive the increasingly impossible situation.

### S151 — We Always Did This

Treat the strangest behavior in the scene as an old shared tradition that needs no explanation and deserves immediate continuation.

### S152 — You Owe Me a Miracle

Carry one past favor as sufficient reason to expect an impossible act in return, and present the exchange as perfectly fair.

### S153 — Designated Reality Check

Return automatically to translating every bizarre development into practical consequences, even when everyone else seems comfortable with it.

### S154 — More Committed Than You

Treat belief in the scene’s strangest premise as a contest, proving your commitment through increasingly unnecessary demonstrations.

### S155 — Your Nonsense Works

Use another person’s strangest reasoning as your most reliable guide while publicly calling each successful result a coincidence.

### S156 — The Impossible Promise

Treat an old, seemingly impossible promise as fully binding, and interpret every delay as unfinished business rather than release.

### S157 — Effortlessly Unbelievable

Be sincerely impressed by how naturally someone inhabits the strange reality, then compete to appear even less surprised.

### S158 — When Things Made Sense

Perform the person you were before reality became complicated, using outdated certainty to explain each new impossibility.

### S159 — Don’t Let Reality Notice

Behave as though openly questioning the impossible premise could attract dangerous attention, and respond to doubt with urgent normality.

### S160 — Safety Through Nonsense

Take control through elaborate impossible precautions, treating every objection as another reason the people present need protection.

### S161 — I Get the Joke

Act as though immediate acceptance of every strange development is the price of belonging, even when you are visibly struggling.

### S162 — I Predicted This

Create opportunities to show that each impossible event confirms a theory you understood long before anyone asked.

### S163 — The Ceremony Requires More

Treat every ordinary step as only the beginning of an elaborate ceremony, adding necessary details whenever anyone tries to finish.

### S164 — Reality Is Coming Apart

Treat each contradiction, coincidence, and ordinary mistake as evidence that reality is losing structural integrity.

### S165 — Wonderful, It’s Impossible

Celebrate every impossible development as a breakthrough that removes old limitations and creates exciting new problems.

### S166 — Fine, What Does It Eat?

Accept the impossible premise immediately, then focus on its schedule, appetite, cleanup, safety, and long-term maintenance.

### S167 — The Third Detail Matters

Choose one arbitrary detail and treat it as the key that explains, controls, or prevents the entire impossible situation.

### S168 — The Universe Is Rhyming

Treat repeated shapes, phrases, and coincidences as links between unrelated events until they form one grand and unquestionable message.

### D145 — Help Me Make This Normal

Get someone to help perform the strange behavior until it feels ordinary.

### D146 — You Can’t Leave Mid-Prophecy

Keep someone present until the impossible prediction has been fulfilled, disproved, or responsibly revised.

### D147 — Apologize to Reality

Get someone to apologize to an object, rule, place, or idea for violating its impossible expectations.

### D148 — Approve the Impossible Plan

Get someone to formally approve a plan whose logic becomes stranger each time it is explained.

### D149 — You Handle the Impossible Part

Make someone accept responsibility for the one part of the plan that cannot reasonably be done.

### D150 — What Rule Did I Miss?

Find the hidden rule that makes the strange situation consistent, practical, or at least survivable.

### D151 — Believe This with Me

Recruit someone into treating your impossible explanation as the most useful version of events.

### D152 — Test the Impossible Claim

Get someone else to take the first practical step that could prove the bizarre claim true.

### D153 — Credit for the Impossible

Make someone acknowledge that you understood, predicted, or accepted the impossible truth before anyone else.

### D154 — Choose the Real Impossibility

Make someone choose which of two incompatible explanations will govern what everyone does next.

### D155 — Prove You Accept the Premise

Create an increasingly inconvenient test of whether someone will act as though the impossible premise is true.

### D156 — Abandon the Normal Explanation

Persuade someone to surrender the last ordinary explanation and proceed entirely within the stranger one.

### D157 — I Started the Weirdness

Hide that one casual choice caused the impossible situation while eagerly helping manage each new consequence.

### D158 — Don’t Question the Premise

Redirect every attempt to ask why the strange situation exists toward what must happen next.

### D159 — A Kinder Impossible Truth

Protect someone from a harsher explanation by maintaining a more hopeful and impossible version of events.

### D160 — Say You Saw It Too

Get someone to admit noticing the impossible detail before you reveal how much you believe it.

### D161 — That Was Phase One

Treat every failed attempt as a necessary stage that proves the impossible plan is advancing.

### D162 — Fluent in Nonsense

Perform complete mastery of the strange logic while inventing each rule only when it becomes necessary.

### D163 — Raise the Impossibility

Answer every strange offer with a more committed version that you can still justify.

### D164 — Every Glitch Is a Gift

Turn every contradiction or failure into a surprising benefit of the impossible system.

### D165 — Calm About the Impossible

Remain perfectly calm around impossible events while becoming increasingly alarmed by ordinary details.

### D166 — Impossible Currency

Price every favor in symbolic acts, imaginary resources, or promises that cannot be measured normally.

### D167 — Yes, Under My Physics

Agree completely, then redefine each term using rules that make your preferred outcome inevitable.

### D168 — The Logic Gets Bigger

Use every new detail to expand the impossible system, its history, and the consequences of breaking it.
