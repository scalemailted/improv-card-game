## S41-single-a — retained

Make yourself constantly useful and read each response as evidence of whether you still belong.

Mechanism: A continually offers useful work and interprets B's responses as evidence that continued belonging must be earned.

Confusable concept: Compulsive tidiness.

**A:** I've swept your kitchen. Anything else before I go?

**A:** The chair could use a polish first.

**B:** I invited you for company.

**A:** Good. I'll talk while I polish. Two reasons to invite me again.

### after

**A:** I've swept your kitchen. Anything else before I go?

**B:** Just sit down. You're my guest.

**A:** The chair could use a polish first.

**B:** I invited you for company.

**A:** Good. I'll talk while I polish. Two reasons to invite me again.

First / second pass: 90 / 92

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A3 turns permission to sit into polishing; A5 explicitly connects usefulness to being invited again. Result 5 — A3 turns the offered chair into another job; A5 explicitly treats usefulness as grounds for being invited again.
- responseLogic: original 5 — B2 supplies the chair for A3; B4's request for company becomes A5's combined talking and polishing. Result 5 — B2's invitation to sit supplies A3's chair; B4's request for company supplies A5's additional service of conversation.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 calls A a guest and B4 asks for company, establishing a host who wants companionship. Result 4 — B2 calls A a guest and B4 wants company, establishing a host whose affection A struggles to accept.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 adds companionship to cleaning and counts both as reasons for another invitation. Result 4 — A5 combines talking and polishing into 'Two reasons to invite me again,' expanding the service rather than accepting reassurance.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 establishes a visiting guest who has swept the kitchen; A3 supplies a playable chair. Result 5 — A1 establishes a guest sweeping the host's kitchen; B2 and A3 make the offered seat immediately playable.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A5's 'Two reasons to invite me again' economically exposes the insecurity behind A1's help. Result 4 — A3's chair polish is economical; A5's 'Two reasons' earns its joke through the guest's belonging anxiety.

Alternatives: Alternative a concerns friendship and hospitality; b concerns employment security and turns reassurance into something to file.

Reservation: No rewrite needed. A5 states the belonging calculation explicitly, but that clarity remains playable.
