## S88-single-a — revised

Treat close monitoring of choices, moods, and details as care, becoming more involved whenever you feel uncertain.

Mechanism: S88 makes A express care through monitoring and respond to uncertainty by increasing involvement.

Confusable concept: Generic control demands obedience without needing uncertainty to expand a sincerely protective role.

**B:** It's only the cinema.

**A:** Dark rooms deserve accurate updates.

**B:** I can look after myself.

**A:** Excellent. Text me when you've started doing that.

### after

**A:** Text when you and Ben reach the cinema, love, so I know you're safe.

**A:** When does it finish? I'll wait up for your message.

**B:** We're walking to dinner afterwards. We haven't chosen anywhere.

**A:** I'll drive behind you while you choose. You can keep your phone off; I'll see for myself that you're having a nice time.
