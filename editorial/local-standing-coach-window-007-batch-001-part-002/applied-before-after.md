## D93-single-a — revised

Remain warm and familiar while letting old resentment sharpen beneath every caring gesture.

Mechanism: A continues familiar care while using that care to deliver an old grievance indirectly.

Confusable concept: A purely hostile sarcastic attack would dispense with the real attention to B's comfort.

### before

**A:** Here's your tea, exactly as you like it. Someone remembers preferences.

**B:** Thank you for remembering.

**A:** You're welcome. The department never closed.

**B:** Are we talking about my forgetting your birthday?

**A:** No. Your tea would get cold during that annual discussion.

### after

**A:** Here's your tea, love. Exactly right, even without a birthday reminder.

**B:** I've brought cake. A month late, I know.

**A:** Your favourite, too. I'll warm your plate; I've kept the candle ready.

**B:** Put it in. I'll sing now.

**A:** Come closer, love. I'll shield the flame. We don't want it stopping halfway through like last year's birthday call.

First / second pass: 86 / 88

- cardFidelity: original 4 — A1 remembers B's tea while needling forgetfulness; A5 protects the tea while reviving the birthday grievance. Result 5 — A1 couples tea and 'love' with a forgotten birthday; A3 warms a plate while exposing the delay; A5 shields the flame while recalling the curtailed call.
- responseLogic: original 5 — B2 thanks A for remembering, prompting A3's ongoing-service claim; B4 names the birthday, enabling A5's annual jab. Result 4 — B2's late cake prompts A3's plate and saved candle; B4's offer to sing prompts A5's protection against another interrupted birthday observance.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 offers genuine thanks and B4 recognizes the shared grievance rather than merely feeding a joke. Result 4 — B2 brings a belated repair and B4 volunteers to sing; A3 and A5 accept that affection while keeping the grievances active.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 turns B4's single forgotten birthday into an 'annual discussion' while maintaining the caring pretext. Result 4 — B4 offers immediate repair; A5 turns helping that repair succeed into a reminder that the neglect extends to last year.
- specificity: original 5 — A1's preferred tea and B4's forgotten birthday establish a readily playable domestic history. Result 5 — A1 establishes intimate tea service; B2 supplies a month-late cake, and A3's saved candle gives actors a concrete shared birthday ritual.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A5 joins keeping tea warm to the duration of the grievance; A3's department metaphor is slightly conspicuous. Result 4 — A3's 'Your favourite, too' economically adds another grievance; A5's flame comparison earns its sting but spells out the parallel.

B4 causes A5: B4 agrees to the candle and offers to sing; A5 draws B closer and protects the flame while invoking an earlier interrupted celebration.

Alternatives: The preserved alternative uses reciprocal physical care and a pickup grievance; this candidate tests a belated birthday repair against accumulated neglect.

Reservation: A3 leaves the cake's flavour implicit, and A5's explicit comparison is slightly polished. No rewrite needed for an internal pass.

## D94-single-b — revised

Price closeness, forgiveness, and support in favors, sacrifices, access, and future loyalty.

Mechanism: A turns renewed closeness and household access into a graduated exchange for reliable attention.

Confusable concept: Setting a reliability boundary could explain A1, but charging coffee for a preliminary discussion creates a relational price schedule.

### before

**A:** You can have the spare key again for one reliable Sunday visit a month.

**B:** You're charging rent on closeness.

**A:** A very modest calendar-based rate.

**B:** Could we start with coffee?

**A:** Certainly. Coffee earns a provisional key discussion.

### after

**A:** You can have the spare key again for one reliable Sunday visit a month.

**B:** I need to collect my things. I work Sundays.

**A:** Saturday lunch buys the hallway. Your records are in the sitting room.

**B:** I'll bring lunch for both of us.

**A:** That gets you the records. Stay to listen to one with me and I'll help carry the boxes.

First / second pass: 90 / 92

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 prices a spare key in monthly visits; A5 assigns coffee a smaller relational return. Result 5 — A1 prices renewed key access in visits; A3 prices hallway access in lunch; A5 exchanges practical support for additional companionship.
- responseLogic: original 5 — A3 accepts B2's rent framing; A5 recalculates access around B4's coffee counteroffer. Result 5 — B2's Sunday work and belongings change A3's terms to Saturday and room access; B4's lunch offer purchases records in A5 and creates a further bargain.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 challenges the price and B4 proposes a lower-commitment way to reconnect. Result 4 — B2 wants possessions and has a work constraint; B4 negotiates through a shared meal, exposing A5's wish to prolong their time together.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 extends the ledger to charging for a discussion before the key itself. Result 4 — After B4 meets the lunch price, A5 makes listening together the price of help carrying boxes, extending the transaction into departure.
- specificity: original 4 — A1's spare key 'again' and Sunday schedule imply a concrete history of withdrawn access. Result 5 — A1's spare key and B2's collection visit establish strained access; A3's hallway and records make the bargaining immediately playable.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A5's 'provisional key discussion' is a precise smaller purchase in response to B4's coffee. Result 4 — A3's 'Saturday lunch buys the hallway' compresses the game; A5's record-listening condition reveals the emotional motive without explaining it.

B4 causes A5: B4 offers lunch for both; A5 credits that payment toward the records and offers carrying help for staying longer.

Alternatives: The preserved alternative sells forgiveness through exhibition labour and family discretion; this candidate sells domestic access and support to secure continued companionship.

Reservation: The relationship is unspecified, and withholding belongings can make A controlling; the bargaining remains clear and playable. No rewrite needed.

## D97-single-a — revised

Get someone to carry part of the emotional burden without making you name how heavy it has become.

Mechanism: A recruits practical help as a share of emotional weight while avoiding a declaration of how overwhelmed they are.

Confusable concept: Start the Hard Part delegates a difficult beginning; this exchange also negotiates ongoing support without requiring disclosure.

### before

**A:** Could you make this one call for me? Just say the booking's cancelled.

**B:** What's wrong?

**A:** Please let the call be the first thing you take off me.

**B:** Do you want to talk afterward?

**A:** Maybe. One thing lighter might make room.

### after

**A:** Could you cancel our anniversary table? The restaurant number's on the fridge.

**B:** What's happened?

**A:** Please let the call be the first thing you take off me.

**B:** I'll call. They might ask whether you want another date.

**A:** Say no for me. Then stay while I put the anniversary glasses away. You can tell me about your day.

First / second pass: 90 / 86

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A3 asks B to remove one burden instead of answering what's wrong; A5 permits support without naming its weight. Result 5 — A1 delegates a painful cancellation; A3 requests relief without answering what happened; A5 asks for company and ordinary conversation during another difficult task.
- responseLogic: original 5 — A3 redirects B2's inquiry toward the call; A5 conditionally accepts B4's offer to talk afterward. Result 4 — B2's inquiry prompts A3 to substitute practical help for disclosure; B4's rescheduling question receives A5's 'Say no for me' before a further request.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 seeks understanding, then B4 offers a gentler sequence rather than demanding disclosure. Result 4 — B2 seeks understanding and B4 accepts the call while anticipating its requirements; A5 trusts B with both an answer and continued presence.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 opens the possibility of further emotional support after the initial practical delegation. Result 4 — B4's possible new date prompts A5 to reject postponement and seek company while putting anniversary glasses away, making the loss more tangible.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 gives B a concrete cancellation call, though the booking's emotional significance remains withheld. Result 4 — A1 provides the restaurant number on the fridge and A5 provides anniversary glasses, although 'our anniversary' leaves B's relationship to the loss uncertain.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A5's 'one thing lighter' develops A3's 'take off me' without explaining the underlying crisis. Result 4 — A3's 'first thing you take off me' carries the emotional request economically; A5's ordinary-day conversation preserves the avoidance without a detachable joke.

B4 causes A5: B4 raises rescheduling; A5 supplies the refusal and turns the cancellation into a request for companionship during the associated packing away.

Alternatives: This candidate delegates a cancellation and then seeks ordinary company; the preserved alternative shares an unfolding letter through controlled pace and physical attention.

Reservation: The referent of 'our anniversary' and B's knowledge require actor interpretation. The practical-to-emotional progression supports a pass without rewriting.

## D98-single-a — revised

Keep someone present until you feel steady enough to face what comes next without them.

Mechanism: A extends B's physical presence in progressively more direct bids for enough steadiness to endure the departure.

Confusable concept: Wanting company indefinitely lacks the card's readiness threshold; A asks for a manageable interval tied to immediate distress.

### before

**A:** Stay for one more tea. My hands haven't caught up with the news.

**B:** We've had three cups.

**A:** Then the fourth needs no explanation.

**B:** My taxi's outside.

**A:** Let me manage one quiet minute while it's still only outside.

### after

**A:** Stay for tea. I need to tell the children about tomorrow's operation without crying.

**B:** We've had three cups.

**A:** Then skip the kettle. Let me practise telling you before I fetch them.

**B:** My taxi's outside.

**A:** Ask for two minutes. Let me say 'I'll be in hospital' without apologising to you. Then I'll try telling them myself.

First / second pass: 90 / 92

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 links staying to unsteady hands after news; A5 asks for one manageable minute before B leaves. Result 5 — A1 asks B to stay until A can tell the children; A3 substitutes rehearsal for tea; A5 requests a final supported attempt before speaking alone.
- responseLogic: original 5 — A3 uses B2's three cups to normalize a fourth; A5 reduces the request when B4 announces the taxi. Result 5 — B2's three cups cause A3 to abandon the kettle; B4's waiting taxi causes A5 to request two minutes and one specific rehearsal sentence.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 registers the extended stay and B4 supplies a real departure constraint. Result 4 — B2 signals that the visit has already stretched and B4 introduces an actual departure constraint; A3 and A5 adapt their requests.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 drops the tea pretext and makes B's imminent departure the explicit pressure on becoming steady. Result 4 — Under B4's taxi pressure, A5 stops extending hospitality and attempts the exact hospital announcement with a concrete emotional hurdle.
- specificity: original 4 — A1's hands and tea provide physical anchors; B4's taxi makes departure immediate. Result 5 — A1 establishes tomorrow's operation and the waiting conversation with the children; A5 supplies 'I'll be in hospital' as immediately playable rehearsal.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's fourth-cup logic grows from B2; A5's 'still only outside' precisely locates the remaining reprieve. Result 4 — B2's 'three cups' economically exposes the delaying tactic; A3's 'skip the kettle' changes tactics cleanly, while A5 is somewhat more explanatory.

B4 causes A5: B4 makes departure imminent; A5 bargains for a short delay and narrows the support needed to saying one sentence.

Alternatives: This candidate uses rehearsal under a taxi deadline; the preserved alternative uses breathing and household actions to tolerate being alone in a new flat.

Reservation: A5 introduces apologising as a second emotional hurdle alongside crying, and B's role is principally departure pressure. No rewrite needed.

## D100-single-a — revised

Get someone to reassure you that your effort, choice, or survival was enough.

Mechanism: A seeks reassurance that the completed effort is sufficient without making it a prelude to further demands.

Confusable concept: Seeking praise for quality would ask whether the painting is good; A asks whether doing this much is enough.

### before

**A:** I only finished one painting. Tell me that counts.

**B:** You could do more next month.

**A:** Before next month, could this one be enough for today?

**B:** You worked hard on it.

**A:** Good. Let me hear that without an assignment afterward.

### after

**A:** I only finished one painting for our exhibition. Tell me I've done enough.

**B:** I'll spread mine out. Nobody will notice the gap.

**A:** I need to know I've contributed, not that you can hide where I haven't.

**B:** Yours belongs in the middle. It's the one people will see first.

**A:** Then introduce me as the other painter. No 'despite everything'. I want to hear that I belong here.

First / second pass: 90 / 86

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 asks whether one painting counts; A3 asks for present sufficiency rather than future productivity. Result 4 — A1 explicitly asks whether one painting is enough; A3 rejects concealment as reassurance; A5 seeks public recognition, with a slight shift toward belonging.
- responseLogic: original 5 — A3 uses B2's next-month suggestion; A5 takes B4's praise and asks to preserve it without another assignment. Result 5 — B2's plan to hide the gap causes A3's distinction between contribution and concealment; B4's central placement causes A5 to request an equal introduction.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 initially motivates through improvement, then B4 changes to recognizing effort. Result 4 — B2 tries to solve their shared exhibition problem and B4 then affirms A's work through prominence; A3 and A5 clarify what kind of support is needed.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 specifies that reassurance must stand alone, building on B4's partial recognition. Result 4 — B4 makes A's painting the first viewers will see; A5 extends that visible endorsement into a public introduction without mitigating language.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 gives one finished painting and B2 supplies the competing monthly productivity frame. Result 5 — A1 establishes a shared exhibition with one completed painting; B2's spacing and B4's central placement make the practical and emotional stakes concrete.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A5's 'assignment afterward' compresses A's concern about B2's advice into a concrete final condition. Result 4 — A3 precisely exposes why hiding a gap is inadequate reassurance; A5's 'No despite everything' efficiently identifies the unwanted qualification.

B4 causes A5: B4 offers the painting pride of place; A5 asks B to make that endorsement audible by introducing A as the other painter.

Alternatives: This candidate seeks equal public recognition for limited output; the preserved alternative seeks permission to rest after enduring a speech despite an omission.

Reservation: A5 edges toward a belonging drive, and 'despite everything' implies unsupplied hardship. The established contribution question keeps it sufficiently anchored; no rewrite needed.
