## S144-single-a — revised

Create several explanations for what is happening. Test each new clue against them, and switch allegiance whenever one theory gains strength.

Mechanism: A maintains rival explanations and shifts support as each new clue changes their relative strength.

Confusable concept: Serial accusation replaces one suspect with another without testing explanations; A explicitly retains competing theories.

### before

**A:** The missing cake is theft, accident, or the dog. Keep all three on the board.

**B:** There's icing on the dog.

**A:** Dog moves into first place.

**B:** There's icing on you too.

**A:** Then theft deserves renewed funding.

### after

**A:** Our wedding cake's gone: theft, accident, or the dog. I suspect theft.

**B:** There's icing on him. Get him outside before Mum sees.

**A:** Dog leads. I'll take him; check under the table for crumbs.

**B:** That icing's from yesterday. The crumbs lead into the cloakroom.

**A:** Theft leads again. Keep the dog here; let's see whether that trail ends at a dropped cake or somebody's coat.

First / second pass: 86 / 85

- cardFidelity: original 5 — A1 offers three theories; A3 favours dog and A5 renews theft after different icing evidence. Result 5 — A1 creates theft, accident and dog explanations; A3 switches to dog on B2's icing evidence, and A5 returns to theft after B4's correction.
- responseLogic: original 4 — B2's icing supports A3's dog theory; B4's icing on A supports A5's theft switch, though not uniquely. Result 4 — A3 acts on B2's icing and requests crumbs; B4 dates the icing and supplies a trail, which A5 follows while retaining accident as a possibility.
- relationship: original 4 — B4 implicates A personally, preventing A from treating evidence as safely external. Result 4 — B2 wants the dog removed before Mum sees; A3 cooperates, but B4's evidence makes A5 reverse that practical decision and recruit B to investigate.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 continues allocating support to theories even when B4 makes A a possible culprit. Result 3 — A5 reverses the dog-removal plan and proposes checking coats, but the stakes and commitment increase only slightly beyond A3's crumb search.
- specificity: original 4 — A1's missing cake and B2/B4's visible icing are immediately playable evidence. Result 5 — A1 establishes a missing wedding cake; B2 adds Mum's impending reaction, and B4 supplies a playable trail into the cloakroom.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A5's 'renewed funding' extends A1's board language while accepting incriminating evidence. Result 4 — A3's 'Dog leads' and A5's 'Theft leads again' efficiently express shifting allegiance, with the dog's changing instructions providing a small comic consequence.

B4 causes A5: B4 discredits the icing and redirects the search to the cloakroom; A5 changes allegiance, reverses the dog's removal and tests the trail's destination.

Alternatives: The preserved lights alternative uses cooperative repair and a safety discovery to revise causal responsibility. This candidate makes theory changes interfere with managing a dog during wedding preparations.

Reservation: Narrow internal pass. A5's preference for theft is provisional rather than strongly proven, and heightening is modest; its proposed test nevertheless keeps both theft and accident active. No rewrite needed.

## S144-single-b — revised

Create several explanations for what is happening. Test each new clue against them, and switch allegiance whenever one theory gains strength.

Mechanism: A lets new clues transfer allegiance among several live explanations and determine the next inquiry.

Confusable concept: Simple elimination reaches an answer once; this stance remains willing to reorder previously considered explanations.

### before

**A:** The lights failed because of weather, wiring, or the caretaker's shortcut.

**B:** It's sunny.

**A:** Weather withdraws gracefully.

**B:** The caretaker unplugged them.

**A:** Shortcut wins. Wiring can keep its dignity.

### after

**A:** The hall lights failed: weather, wiring, or the caretaker saving electricity. Wiring's my favourite.

**B:** He unplugged them. Help me reach the socket.

**A:** Economy takes the lead. I'll hold your ladder.

**B:** The socket's wet. There's rain coming through the roof.

**A:** Weather overtakes him. Leave the plug; let's ask whether he unplugged it before the leak.

First / second pass: 66 / 92

- cardFidelity: original 4 — A1 supplies three explanations and A3/A5 eliminate them, but only A5 clearly chooses an allegiance. Result 5 — A1 prefers wiring among three theories; A3 switches to economy; A5 switches to weather and asks a discriminating question.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 uses B2's sunshine; A5 uses B4's unplugging, although unplugging does not establish a shortcut motive. Result 5 — B2's unplugging changes A3's theory and action; B4's rain changes A5's theory and cancels reconnecting.
- relationship: original 2 — B2 and B4 dispense answers without a practical want or changing tactic. Result 4 — B2 wants help reaching the socket, giving A3 a useful supporting role before B4 changes the task.
- heightening: original 2 — A5 announces the answer already supplied in B4 rather than developing the competing-theory behaviour. Result 4 — A5 makes theory revision consequential by stopping the plug attempt and investigating the caretaker's timing.
- specificity: original 3 — A1 establishes failed lights but no occasion, location, or urgency. Result 5 — A1 locates failed hall lights; B2 supplies the socket, A3 the ladder, and B4 the roof leak.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's graceful withdrawal and A5's dignity are concise, but mostly decorate elimination. Result 4 — A3's 'takes the lead' and A5's 'overtakes' keep the competing-theory voice while advancing the repair.

B4 causes A5: B4 discovers rain at the socket; A5 backs weather, stops reconnection and questions the unplugging sequence.

Alternatives: B is an active collaborator facing a changing repair problem, unlike the cake alternative's mostly evidentiary role.

Reservation: The ranking language is slightly stylized, but the practical interaction supports it. No rewrite needed.
