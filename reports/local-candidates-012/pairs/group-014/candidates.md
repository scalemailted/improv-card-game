## S01+D67-a

**S01 Top of the Ladder:** Carry yourself as the highest-status person present. Treat questions as requests for your approval.

**D67 Rank Everything:** Turn every new contribution into a ranking that places you nearer the top.

**A1:** I'll decide how we handle this. I planned this picnic, so I get first choice of cake.

**B2:** I drove everyone here.

**A3:** You can question the details after my decision. Destination selection outranks transport. You can go second.

**B4:** I baked it. The baker gets the big end piece.

**A5:** Baking outranks driving. But I commissioned the cake, so commissioning goes first. I'll take the end piece.

Local writer total: 90. evidence-complete-awaiting-fresh-pair-review.

Mechanism: A appoints themself judge of contributions and grants B a lower cake priority after re-ranking each claimed service.

Alternative distinction: Allocation of cake based on picnic services differs from a project report ranking printer work.

Reservation: Retained; the ranking changes the actual cake choice, not only labels.
