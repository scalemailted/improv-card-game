## S235-single-a — revised

Treat one unexplained recurring action as essential. Allow its meaning to change each time it returns.

Mechanism: A insists on a recurring cup-tapping ritual whose purpose changes from unexplained necessity to teasing continuity to room for remembrance.

Confusable concept: Explaining a family custom can leave its meaning fixed; this card renews the same action with a different purpose on each return.

### before

**A:** Before we eat, tap your cup twice. Mum always did.

**B:** For good luck?

**A:** That was what she told guests. We knew it meant the tea was weak.

**B:** This tea is strong.

**A:** Then today the taps can mean we wish she were here to complain.

### after

**A:** Wait, don't drink yet. Two taps on the cup first.

**B:** For luck? That's good tea.

**A:** Two taps meant Mum had made it weak. We'd better tap again; she'll think we've gone polite.

**B:** I miss her complaints. The table's too quiet.

**A:** Two more, then. These are to leave her a turn. We can wait before we drink.

First / second pass: 80 / 95

- cardFidelity: original 3 — A1 requests taps and A5 changes their meaning, but A3 explains historical meanings without making the action recur. Result 5 — A1 requires unexplained cup taps; A3 makes them familial criticism and renewed irreverence; A5 makes them space for Mum's absent reply.
- responseLogic: original 5 — B2's luck question produces A3's private explanation; B4's strong tea causes A5's memorial reinterpretation. Result 5 — B2's luck guess and praise prompt A3's weak-tea explanation; B4's missing complaints prompt A5's reserved conversational turn.
- relationship: original 4 — B2 seeks entry into a family custom and B4 challenges its relevance to the tea being served. Result 5 — B4 shares grief and a specific wish for Mum's complaints, changing A's ritual from teasing remembrance into shared listening.
- heightening: original 4 — A5 moves from a complaint about tea to missing Mum because B4 removes the original complaint. Result 4 — A5 gives the same taps greater emotional consequence by asking both people to leave a turn for someone absent.
- specificity: original 5 — A1's cup taps and A3's weak tea make a clear domestic ritual. Result 5 — A1 establishes tea about to be drunk; A3's weak tea and B4's quiet table ground the family history.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3's guest-facing luck explanation contrasts economically with the family's complaint signal. Result 4 — A3's fear of having 'gone polite' earns affectionate wit; A5's explanation of waiting is slightly extended but playable.

B4 causes A5: B4 misses Mum's complaints and the table's sound; A5 makes further taps leave room for her turn.

Alternatives: Alternative a develops an intimate shared memory; b uses institutional rehearsal custom to address interruptions and welcome a newcomer.

Reservation: No rewrite needed. The later ritual meanings are spoken explicitly, so performance must preserve sincerity rather than explain symbolism.

## S235-single-b — revised

Treat one unexplained recurring action as essential. Allow its meaning to change each time it returns.

Mechanism: A insists on unexplained double knocking, then renews it as a declaration of readiness and a promise of continued support.

Confusable concept: Ordinary door knocking requests entry; this card makes the already-answered knock recur with purposes that change as B needs courage and reassurance.

### before

**A:** I always turn this chair toward the door before rehearsal.

**B:** To welcome whoever's late?

**A:** Originally. Then it reminded the late person everyone had noticed.

**B:** Nobody's late today.

**A:** Then it can wait for the member we lost. Leave it facing outward.

### after

**A:** Before your audition, knock twice on this door. We can't skip that.

**B:** They knocked back. Can we go in? My hands are shaking.

**A:** Twice again. Now we're saying we're ready, even if your hands aren't.

**B:** They're calling me in alone. How will I know you're still here?

**A:** I'll knock twice when your song ends. This time it means I'm staying until you come out.

First / second pass: 68 / 88

- cardFidelity: original 3 — A1 describes a recurring chair placement, but A3 and A5 mostly explain it rather than return to the action. Result 5 — A1 makes two unexplained knocks essential: 'We can't skip that.' A3 repeats them as readiness; A5 repeats them as a promise to stay. The same action acquires different meanings.
- responseLogic: original 4 — B2 supplies welcoming lateness for A3 to darken; B4 removes lateness, though A5 introduces a previously unmentioned loss. Result 4 — B2's answering knocks and shaking hands prompt A3's declaration of readiness despite fear. B4's separation anxiety prompts A5's promised signal, though its audibility during an audition is assumed.
- relationship: original 3 — B2 and B4 ask about the ritual but have no expressed rehearsal need or personal response to the absent member. Result 4 — B2 wants to enter despite nerves; B4 needs assurance that A will remain outside. A3 and A5 adapt the ritual to those distinct needs, establishing a supportive relationship.
- heightening: original 3 — A5 introduces bereavement after B4's attendance correction, supplying gravity more than developed interaction. Result 4 — B4 removes A's ability to accompany B. A5 extends the ritual beyond the shared doorway moment into a commitment to remain until B returns.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 establishes a rehearsal room, a chair and a door, making the staging understandable. Result 5 — A1 establishes an audition doorway and a concrete two-knock action. B2 supplies shaking hands and an answer from inside; B4 establishes that B must enter alone.
- brevityWit: original 4 — A3 economically turns a welcome into a public reminder of lateness. Result 4 — A3's 'even if your hands aren't' gives the reassurance a concise character-based turn. A5 earns its warmth through B4's question, although the exchange offers more tenderness than comic surprise.

B4 causes A5: B4 must enter alone and asks how to know A remains. A5 makes the next two knocks a reassurance signal and promises to stay until B comes out.

Alternatives: The preserved alternative moves through family memory and shared grief around tea. This candidate uses anticipatory nerves and reassurance between an auditioning person and a supporter; its final recurrence promises continued presence rather than making room for someone absent.

Reservation: The song-end signal assumes B can hear A's knocks and A can hear the song finish. This is a minor staging assumption. No rewrite is needed.
