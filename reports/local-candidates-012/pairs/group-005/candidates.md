**B2:** Can I have a five-minute rate? That's all I need.

**A3:** One lift is authorized. You may add a compliment about my roses for the missing journey.

**B4:** Can I just pay cash?

**A5:** Cash is approved at five pounds. I'll permit a two-pound reduction if you say we're still friends while handing it over.

Mechanism: A grants a ladder loan and changes its price from an assumed superior position, making even reassurance a negotiable concession.

Alternative distinction: The neighbor loan trades practical and emotional currencies; the apology alternative trades holiday time for genuine forgiveness.

Reservation: The approved application wording is intentionally pompous, while the actual loan terms remain clear.
