> **v0.18.0 editorial consolidation:** Stable card IDs are preserved. The selected-card copy below reflects the current v0.18.0 wording; the original release wording remains recorded in `editorial/v0.18.0/revision-ledger.json`.


**Pack:** Relationship Knots  
**Release target:** Imprompt v0.11.0  
**Pool:** 32 Stance candidates + 32 Drive candidates  
**Disposition:** 48 selected, 8 held, 8 rejected

This document records the larger drafting pool from which S73–S96 and D73–D96 were selected. Held and rejected ideas remain documented so future revisions do not unknowingly repeat discarded concepts.
