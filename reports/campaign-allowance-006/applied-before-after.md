## S111-single-a — revised

Treat pauses, changes, and uncertainty as signs that rejection or loss is approaching, then prepare yourself before it arrives.

Mechanism: A interprets changes and uncertainty as approaching loss and prepares before that loss arrives.

Confusable concept: General pessimism can anticipate mistakes without treating a present cue as evidence of impending rejection.

### before

**A:** I've brought a box to the meeting. Is this where you dismiss me?

**B:** We want to discuss your promotion.

**A:** Promotion to what? The box is very adaptable.

**B:** More responsibility.

**A:** Right. I'll keep it for the mistakes.

### after

**A:** You moved our meeting to Friday. I've brought a box for my desk.

**B:** It's a promotion. You'll manage the new branch.

**A:** Away from everyone? I'll pack before they have to say goodbye.

**B:** You can still come to Friday drinks.

**A:** I'll bring my own glass. Better get used to being a visitor before they stop inviting me.

First / second pass: 63 / 92

- cardFidelity: original 3 — A1 prepares for dismissal, but A5 prepares for mistakes rather than approaching rejection or loss. Result 5 — A1 treats a rescheduled meeting as impending dismissal; A3 prepares for separation; A5 prepares for eventual exclusion despite reassurance.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 questions B2's promotion; A5 connects B4's responsibility to mistakes, though the box's purpose becomes obscure. Result 5 — B2's new branch makes A3 anticipate leaving colleagues; B4's drinks invitation makes A5 prepare to attend as a visitor.
- relationship: original 3 — B2 offers promotion, but B4's 'More responsibility' supplies little managerial intention or pressure. Result 4 — B2 offers professional advancement and B4 preserves social contact, giving A two credible forms of reassurance to reinterpret.
- heightening: original 2 — A5's box for mistakes weakens A1's concrete preparation for losing the job. Result 4 — A5 moves beyond packing in A3 to rehearsing outsider status and anticipating the withdrawal of invitations.
- specificity: original 4 — A1's meeting and packing box establish a playable employment conversation. Result 5 — A1's Friday meeting and desk box immediately establish a workplace; B2's branch and B4's drinks make the losses concrete.
- brevityWit: original 3 — A3's adaptable box is economical, but A5 makes its use metaphorical rather than playable. Result 4 — A5's own glass economically turns an inclusive invitation into preparation for exclusion, though bringing glassware is slightly contrived.

B4 causes A5: B4 offers continued attendance at drinks; A5 turns that attendance into practice for becoming an unwanted visitor.

Alternatives: The preserved alternative concerns romantic rejection and adapts tissues and containers to dinner plans. This candidate concerns workplace displacement and continued membership of a social group.

Reservation: The personal glass is less natural than the desk box, but the causal chain remains clear. No rewrite needed.
