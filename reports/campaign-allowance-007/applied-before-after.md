## S95-single-a — revised

Choose one phrase from the interaction and keep returning to what its wording must reveal about the relationship.

Mechanism: A repeatedly examines one chosen phrase as involuntary evidence of how conditional B's welcome really is.

Confusable concept: General semantic nitpicking would move among interesting words without holding one phrase to its relational implications.

### before

**A:** Then now has brought an unnecessary suitcase.

**B:** You're reading too much into it.

### after

**A:** Your note says I'm 'welcome for now.' Should I unpack?

**A:** Then why 'for now' instead of 'until the repairs'? Is your welcome wearing out?

**B:** I'll cross it out. You're welcome.

**A:** But 'for now' was there before I complained. What happens when I stop asking?

- cardFidelity: original 3 — A1 selects 'for now', but A5 switches to 'too much' instead of returning to that chosen phrase. Result 5 — A1 selects 'for now'; A3 and A5 keep returning to what those same words reveal about the durability of welcome.
- responseLogic: original 4 — A3 challenges B2's omission of 'now'; A5 uses B4's exact language, although that weakens card fidelity. Result 5 — A3 contrasts B2's repair explanation with the original wording; A5 treats B4's deletion as a response to complaint rather than reassurance.
- relationship: original 3 — B2 clarifies hospitality and B4 resists interpretation, but neither adds a concrete hosting constraint. Result 4 — B2 offers accommodation until repairs and B4 offers to amend the note, giving A substantive attempts at reassurance to reinterpret.
- heightening: original 3 — A5 starts a new wording complaint rather than deepening the original phrase's implications. Result 4 — A5 makes even removal of the phrase evidence that welcome may depend on continued questioning.
- specificity: original 4 — A1 identifies a written welcome and B2 establishes permission to stay. Result 5 — A1's note and unpacking question establish temporary lodging; B2 gives the concrete reason: repairs to A's flat.
- brevityWit: original 3 — A3's suitcase metaphor is apt, but A5's 'measurement' adds another polished abstraction. Result 4 — A3's 'Is your welcome wearing out?' and A5's complaint distinction arise directly from the disputed phrase.

B4 causes A5: B4 offers to cross out the phrase; A5 distinguishes the original intention from an amendment made after objection.

Alternatives: This interrogates a written offer and its amendment; the friends alternative interrogates a spoken qualifier and its claimed reassuring purpose.

Reservation: A's anxious interpretation overlaps with S92, but the sustained exact wording clearly supplies this card's distinctive mechanism. No rewrite needed.
