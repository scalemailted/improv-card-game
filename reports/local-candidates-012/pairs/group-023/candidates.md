## S01+D112-a

**S01 Top of the Ladder:** Carry yourself as the highest-status person present. Treat questions as requests for your approval.

**D112 Admit You’re Hurt:** Get someone to admit hurt, fear, or disappointment before you reveal that you feel it too.

**A1:** I've decided we should discuss Mum cancelling our birthday dinner. Were you disappointed?

**B2:** Were you?

**A3:** You may ask about my feelings once we've established yours.

**B4:** Yes. I cried in the supermarket.

**A5:** Then crying is permitted. I did it at the checkout. You may sit beside me; apparently this discussion requires tissues.

Local writer total: 90. evidence-complete-awaiting-fresh-pair-review.

Mechanism: A makes the other person disclose pain first by controlling whose feelings may be discussed.

Alternative distinction: Family grief under self-appointed control differs from the workplace complaint in alternative b.

Reservation: The invitation remains bossy rather than an explicitly equal relationship; that is the intended Stance, not emotional resolution.
