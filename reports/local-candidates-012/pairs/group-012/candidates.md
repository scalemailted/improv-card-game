## S01+D57-a

**D57 Use the Proper Title:** Make someone acknowledge your standing through a title, privilege, courtesy, or public sign of respect.

**A1:** I'll decide how we handle this. At the staff barbecue, introduce me as Director Patel.

**B2:** I'm introducing everyone by first name. Even the owner.

**A3:** You can question the details after my decision. Then give me a separate introduction when I welcome the staff.

**B4:** The owner's doing the welcome. You offered to serve burgers.

**A5:** Then introduce the burger service: 'Director Patel will serve you.' I'd like them to hear who's making this gesture.

Mechanism: A commands increasingly specific public introductions so even burger service must advertise rank, treating B objections as terms to overrule.

Alternative distinction: Public spoken introduction during service differs from the place-setting dispute in alternative b.

Reservation: Retained despite a clumsy stock prefix; the service-as-gesture ending demonstrates the pair.
